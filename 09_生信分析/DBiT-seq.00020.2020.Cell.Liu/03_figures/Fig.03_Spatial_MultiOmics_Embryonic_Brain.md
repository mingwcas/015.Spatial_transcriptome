# Fig. 3 — Spatial Multi-Omics Mapping of an Embryonic Mouse Brain

## Caption（原文）
> (A) Bright-field optical image of the brain region of a mouse embryo (E10).
> (B) H&E image of the mouse embryo brain region (E10). It was obtained on an adjacent tissue section.
> (C) Pan-mRNA and pan-protein-panel spatial expression maps of the brain region of a mouse embryo (E10) obtained with 25-μm pixel size. The spatial pattern of whole transcriptome (pan-mRNA) correlated with cell density and morphology in the tissue.
> (D) Spatial expression of four individual proteins: CD63, Pan-endothelial cell antigen (PECA), EpCAM (CD326), and MAdCAM-1. Spatial protein expression heatmaps revealed brain tissue region-specific expression and the brain microvascular network.
> (E) Validation by immunofluorescence staining. Spatial expression of EpCAM and PECA reconstructed from DBiT-seq and the immunofluorescence image of the same proteins were superimposed onto the H&E image for comparison. A highly localized expression pattern of EpCAM is in strong correlation with immunostaining as seen by the line profile. The network of microvasculature revealed by PECA in DBiT-seq is correlated with the immunostaining image.
> (F) Gene expression heatmap of 11 clusters obtained by unsupervised clustering analysis. Top ranked differentially expressed genes are shown in each cluster.
> (G) Spatial map of clusters 1, 2, 5, and 9. GO analysis identified the major biological processes within each cluster, in agreement with anatomical annotations.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — Bright-field Image
**结论**：E10胚胎脑区域明场图像
**关键数据**：提供了组织形态的基准图像

### Panel B — H&E Image
**结论**：相邻组织切片的H&E染色
**关键数据**：用于与空间组学数据叠加对照

### Panel C — Pan-mRNA/Protein Spatial Maps
**结论**：25μm像素成功揭示脑组织细胞密度和形态相关的转录组模式
**关键数据**：pan-mRNA空间模式与组织细胞密度相关

### Panel D — Four Protein Spatial Expression
**结论**：CD63、PECA、EpCAM和MAdCAM-1显示脑区特异性表达模式
**关键数据**：PECA清晰勾勒脑微血管网络；EpCAM在特定区域高表达形成细线状（~25μm）；MAdCAM-1在forebrain亚区差异表达

### Panel E — Immunofluorescence Validation
**结论**：DBiT-seq蛋白检测与免疫荧光染色高度一致
**关键数据**：EpCAM的线状表达模式与免疫染色强相关；PECA显示的微血管网络与免疫染色图像吻合

### Panel F — Gene Expression Heatmap
**结论**：10个clusters显示清晰的差异表达基因模式
**关键数据**：每个cluster有特异性高表达基因

### Panel G — Spatial Cluster Maps
**结论**：Cluster 1富集telencephalon发育，Cluster 2富集红细胞/血管，Cluster 5富集axonogenesis，Cluster 9富集cardiac muscle
**关键数据**：空间cluster映射与解剖学注释一致

## 总体结论
Fig. 3 证明了DBiT-seq在亚组织区域的高分辨率空间多组学映射能力。25μm像素可清晰区分脑微血管网络（单层内皮细胞）和上皮细胞等精细结构，蛋白与mRNA数据互相验证。
