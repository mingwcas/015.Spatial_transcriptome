# Fig. 5 — Global Clustering Analysis of 11 Mouse Embryos from E10, E11, and E12

## Caption（原文）
> (A) tSNE plot showing the clustering analysis of DBiT-seq data from all 11 mouse embryo tissue samples.
> (B) tSNE plot color-coded for different mouse embryo tissue samples.
> (C) Heatmap of differentially expressed genes in 20 clusters and GO analysis. Select GO terms and top ranked genes are shown for the clusters implicated in muscle system, pigment metabolic system, blood vessel development, neuron development, and telencephalon development.
> (D) UMAP plot showing the cluster analysis result, color-coded for different samples (left) or the developmental stages (right).

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — tSNE Overall Clustering
**结论**：11个胚胎样本的DBiT-seq数据聚类分析揭示主要组织类型
**关键数据**：tSNE显示20个clusters

### Panel B — Sample Color Coding
**结论**：来自同一样本的像素倾向于聚在一起
**关键数据**：不同样本用不同颜色标记

### Panel C — Differential Gene Heatmap
**结论**：每个cluster富集特定的生物学过程和基因
**关键数据**：Muscle system (Myh gene family)、pigment metabolic system、blood vessel development、neuron development、telencephalon development等

### Panel D — UMAP by Sample and Stage
**结论**：E10、E11、E12样本沿发育轨迹分布
**关键数据**：左图：不同样本聚类；右图：发育阶段（E10→E12轨迹）

## 总体结论
Fig. 5 展示了DBiT-seq在多样本整合分析中的能力。11个来自不同胚胎阶段（E10-E12）和不同组织区域的样本通过整合分析揭示了发育时间轴上的转录组变化，证明了该技术可用于研究动态发育过程。
