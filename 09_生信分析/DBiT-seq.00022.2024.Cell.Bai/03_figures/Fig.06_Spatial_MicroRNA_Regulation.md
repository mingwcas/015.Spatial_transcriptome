# Fig. 6 — Spatial microRNA regulations in the MALT section

## Caption（原文）
> (A) MicroRNAs detected by Patho-DBiT in the MALT section. The pie chart illustrates the percentage distribution of the detected count number per spatial pixel.
> (B) Spatial distribution of the smooth muscle cell score.
> (C) Spatial mapping of smooth muscle cell-specific miR-143 and miR-145.
> (D) Differentially expressed microRNAs between the tumor and non-tumor regions.
> (E) Regulatory network between the top upregulated microRNAs and the gene expression in the tumor region.
> (F) Spatial expression, read coverage, and regulatory relationship of miR-21.
> (G) Spatial expression, read coverage, and expression comparison between tumor and non-tumor regions of miR-155. Significance level was calculated with two-tailed Mann-Whitney test, **** p < 0.0001.
> (H) Spatial interactions involving miR-155 and its upstream and downstream signaling pathways. The Pearson correlation was calculated across 1,366 spatial pixels within the tumor B-cell region.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — MicroRNA detection
**结论**：Patho-DBiT在MALT组织中检测到大量miRNA
**关键数据**：每个像素检测到的miRNA数量分布

### Panel B — Smooth muscle cell score
**结论**：平滑肌细胞评分显示空间分布模式
**关键数据**：组织结构特异性分布

### Panel C — miR-143 and miR-145
**结论**：平滑肌细胞特异性miRNA空间映射
**关键数据**：miR-143和miR-145空间分布一致

### Panel D — Differentially expressed miRNAs
**结论**：肿瘤和非肿瘤区域存在差异表达的miRNA
**关键数据**：miRNA表达差异显著

### Panel E — Regulatory network
**结论**：构建了miRNA-基因调控网络
**关键数据**：上调miRNA与靶基因的调控关系

### Panel F — miR-21 expression
**结论**：miR-21在肿瘤区域高表达
**关键数据**：空间表达、读段覆盖和调控关系

### Panel G — miR-155 expression
**结论**：miR-155在肿瘤区域显著高表达
**关键数据**：Mann-Whitney检验p < 0.0001

### Panel H — miR-155 signaling
**结论**：miR-155参与上游和下游信号通路
**关键数据**：1,366个肿瘤像素的Pearson相关系数0.52和0.56

## 总体结论
Fig. 6展示了Patho-DBiT在空间分辨的miRNA分析中的应用，揭示了MALT淋巴瘤中miRNA的调控网络，特别是miR-21和miR-155在肿瘤发生中的重要作用，为理解miRNA在淋巴瘤中的功能提供了空间维度信息。
