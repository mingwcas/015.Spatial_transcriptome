# Fig. 4 — High-resolution tissue architecture resolved by integrating Patho-DBiT with histology

## Caption（原文）
> (A) Spatial mapping of the MALT section using 20 μm Patho-DBiT device covering 100,000 spots. Left top: tissue scanning. Left bottom: H&E of an adjacent section. Right: unsupervised clustering.
> (B) Spatial identification of representative cell types.
> (C) IF staining of plasma cell marker (CD138) and macrophage marker (CD68) in the selected regions P and M in (B).
> (D) Representative transcriptomic neighborhoods revealed by Patho-DBiT, closely aligned with H&E tissue morphology.
> (E) Super-resolved high-resolution spatial clustering and representative gene expression enhanced with iStar.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — MALT section mapping
**结论**：使用20 μm Patho-DBiT设备分析MALT淋巴瘤组织
**关键数据**：100,000个检测点，高分辨率空间映射

### Panel B — Cell type identification
**结论**：空间识别多种细胞类型
**关键数据**：基于标记基因的细胞类型注释

### Panel C — IF staining validation
**结论**：免疫荧光验证了浆细胞和巨噬细胞的身份
**关键数据**：CD138和CD68染色结果一致

### Panel D — Transcriptomic neighborhoods
**结论**：转录组邻域与组织形态学一致
**关键数据**：空间结构清晰对应

### Panel E — Super-resolved clustering
**结论**：iStar增强的超分辨率聚类
**关键数据**：分辨率显著提高，空间结构更精细

## 总体结论
Fig. 4展示了Patho-DBiT与组织病理学的整合，实现了高分辨率的空间转录组分析，通过iStar算法进一步提高了空间分辨率，为研究组织微环境提供了精细工具。
