# Fig. 3 — High-sensitivity spatial transcriptomics of an AITL sample stored for 5 years

## Caption（原文）
> (A) Spatial transcriptome mapping of an FFPE AITL tissue stored for 5 years. Left top: H&E of an adjacent section. Left bottom: tissue scanning. Right: unsupervised clustering.
> (B) Top-ranked DEGs defining each cluster.
> (C) Spatial phenotyping of an adjacent section using CODEX technology.
> (D) Spatial distributions of B cells, T cells, and macrophages revealed by Patho-DBiT, exhibiting a strong Pearson correlation with the proteomic data from CODEX.
> (E) Top: CODEX data from the yellow square indicated area in (C). Bottom: DEGs in cluster 0 corresponding to the indicated region.
> (F) Ligand-receptor interactions within cluster 0.
> (G) Signaling pathways regulated by the DEGs in cluster 0. z score is computed and used to reflect the predicted activation level (z > 0, activated; z < 0, inhibited; z ≥ 2 or z ≤ -2 can be considered significant).
> (H) Graphical network of canonical pathways, upstream regulators, and biological functions regulated by DEGs identified in cluster 0.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — AITL tissue mapping
**结论**：Patho-DBiT成功分析5年存档的AITL FFPE组织
**关键数据**：无监督聚类显示清晰的空间结构

### Panel B — DEGs defining clusters
**结论**：每个聚类由特异性差异表达基因定义
**关键数据**：聚类marker基因明确

### Panel C — CODEX spatial phenotyping
**结论**：CODEX蛋白组学验证了转录组结果
**关键数据**：蛋白质表达模式与转录组一致

### Panel D — Cell type distributions
**结论**：Patho-DBiT与CODEX检测的细胞类型分布高度相关
**关键数据**：Pearson相关系数显著

### Panel E — CODEX and DEGs comparison
**结论**：特定区域的蛋白表达与基因表达对应
**关键数据**：黄色方框区域显示恶性Tfh细胞

### Panel F — Ligand-receptor interactions
**结论**：CXCL13与其受体的相互作用在cluster 0中活跃
**关键数据**：配体-受体对显著

### Panel G — Signaling pathways
**结论**：ERK/MAPK通路在cluster 0中激活
**关键数据**：z score显示通路激活状态

### Panel H — Network of pathways
**结论**：构建了差异表达基因调控的信号网络
**关键数据**：上游调控因子和生物功能关联

## 总体结论
Fig. 3证明了Patho-DBiT能够高灵敏度地分析长期存档的FFPE组织，成功识别了AITL中的恶性Tfh细胞群体，并通过CODEX蛋白组学验证了转录组结果，展示了该技术在临床病理学研究中的应用潜力。
