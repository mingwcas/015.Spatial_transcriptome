# Fig. 1 — Patho-DBiT workflow and spatial whole transcriptome mapping of mouse embryo

## Caption（原文）
> (A) Schematic workflow, molecular underpinnings, and technological spectrum of Patho-DBiT.
> (B) Patho-DBiT's performance on an E13 mouse embryo section. Top left: H&E of an adjacent section. Top right: tissue scanning. Bottom: unsupervised clustering.
> (C) Spatial pan-mRNA and UMI count maps.
> (D) Correlation analysis between replicates, with the Pearson coefficient indicated.
> (E) Read coverage along the gene body and the percentage of reads mapped to the 50 UTR.
> (F) Proportion of reads mapped to different RNA categories.
> (G) Comparison of non-coding RNA read mapping ratios between Patho-DBiT and normal DBiT. Data shown are mean ± SD (n = 2).
> (H) Spatial count maps of different non-coding RNAs.
> (I) Left: cell patterning and embedding. Right: relative miR-142 expression in KO and WT cells. Data shown are mean ± SD (n = 3).
> (J) Spatial miR-142 expression in patterned cells (I) and the evaluation of detection sensitivity and specificity.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — Schematic workflow
**结论**：展示了Patho-DBiT的完整工作流程，从FFPE组织处理到空间转录组测序
**关键数据**：整合了原位多聚腺苷酸化、微流控条形码和计算创新

### Panel B — E13 mouse embryo section
**结论**：验证了Patho-DBiT在小鼠胚胎组织中的性能
**关键数据**：H&E染色、组织扫描和无监督聚类结果一致

### Panel C — Spatial count maps
**结论**：展示了全转录组和UMI的空间分布
**关键数据**：空间表达模式与组织结构一致

### Panel D — Correlation analysis
**结论**：重复样本间高度相关
**关键数据**：Pearson相关系数显示高重复性

### Panel E — Read coverage
**结论**：读段覆盖沿基因体分布均匀
**关键数据**：5'UTR读段比例适中

### Panel F — RNA categories
**结论**：检测到多种RNA类型
**关键数据**：47%读段映射到非编码RNA

### Panel G — Non-coding RNA comparison
**结论**：Patho-DBiT比传统DBiT检测更多非编码RNA
**关键数据**：非编码RNA读段比例显著提高

### Panel H — Spatial non-coding RNA maps
**结论**：非编码RNA具有空间特异性表达模式
**关键数据**：tRNA、snRNA等空间分布图

### Panel I — Cell patterning and miR-142
**结论**：验证了miR-142在敲除细胞中的表达
**关键数据**：KO和WT细胞中miR-142表达差异显著

### Panel J — Detection sensitivity and specificity
**结论**：Patho-DBiT具有高检测灵敏度和特异性
**关键数据**：灵敏度86.95%，特异性84.19%

## 总体结论
Fig. 1全面展示了Patho-DBiT技术的工作流程和性能验证，证明该技术能够在FFPE组织中实现高灵敏度、高特异性的空间全转录组测序，包括传统方法难以检测的非编码RNA。
