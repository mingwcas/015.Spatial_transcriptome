# Fig. 5 — Genome-wide spatial variant profiling for differentiating malignant subclones

## Caption（原文）
> (A) Molecular underpinnings of detecting variations printed in pre-mRNA transcripts.
> (B) Comparison of genomic location coverage bandwidth between Patho-DBiT and scRNA-seq datasets.
> (C) Spatial expression map of accumulated SNVs burden.
> (D) IHC staining of BCL-2 and CD43 commonly used for immunophenotyping MALT tumor cells.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — Molecular underpinnings
**结论**：展示了从pre-mRNA中检测变异的分子机制
**关键数据**：pre-mRNA含有内含子，可检测更多变异

### Panel B — Genomic coverage comparison
**结论**：Patho-DBiT比scRNA-seq覆盖更多基因组区域
**关键数据**：覆盖带宽显著更宽

### Panel C — Spatial SNV burden
**结论**：SNV负担在空间上分布不均
**关键数据**：B细胞区域显示更高的SNV负担

### Panel D — IHC staining
**结论**：BCL-2和CD43免疫组化验证了肿瘤细胞分布
**关键数据**：蛋白表达与转录组一致

## 总体结论
Fig. 5展示了Patho-DBiT在全基因组范围内检测RNA变异的能力，通过空间分辨的SNV分析成功区分了恶性亚克隆，为理解肿瘤异质性和演化提供了新工具。
