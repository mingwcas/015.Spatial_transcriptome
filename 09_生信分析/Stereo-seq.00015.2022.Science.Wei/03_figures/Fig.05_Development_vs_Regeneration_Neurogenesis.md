# Fig. 5 — Comparison of neurogenesis between development and regeneration

## Caption（原文）
> (A and B) Spatial distribution of major cell types in the dorsal pallium of the left hemisphere at 15 DPI–4 (A) and at St.44, St.54, St.57, and Juv. (B).
> (C) Heatmap displaying the correlation between different EGC types at 15 DPI and four developmental stages.
> (D) Spatial distribution of cell types in the dorsal pallium of the axolotl left telencephalon at St.57. Cells potentially involved in the development of nptxEX are framed by the white line.
> (E) Spatially visualized expression of key markers on the section shown in (D).
> (F) RNA velocity streamline plot showing the predicted dorsal pallium development trajectory in the left hemisphere of the axolotl telencephalon at St.57.
> (G and H) RNA velocity streamline plot showing the predicted lineage transition trajectory of dEGC, dNBL, immature nptxEX, and nptxEX at St.57 (G) and reaEGC, rIPC1, IMN, and nptxEX at 15 DPI (H).
> (I) Left: Heatmap of gene expression during nptxEX development and regeneration. Eight distinct groups of genes were sorted by their dynamic expression pattern. Right: Line plot depicting standardized Stereo-seq signal by fuzzy cluster analysis for eight groups, with green lines representing the expression dynamics of individual genes and the orange line representing the integrated pattern of each group of genes at St.57 or 15 DPI. Dev. Up, up-regulated in development; Dev. Down, down-regulated in development; Reg. Up, up-regulated in regeneration; Reg. Down, down-regulated in regeneration; None, no significant change.
> (J) Bar plot exhibiting the representative Gene Ontology enrichment pathways of groups in (I).

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A and B — Spatial Distribution Comparison
**结论**：发育和再生过程中背侧pallium的细胞类型空间分布模式相似：dEGC、dNBL、immature nptxEX、nptxEX从VZ到pallium区域呈阶梯状排列。

**关键数据**：St.57出现成熟nptxEX时，四种细胞类型排列模式与15 DPI-4截面匹配，提示nptxEX再生可能重演nptxEX发育过程。

### Panel C — EGC Type Correlation Heatmap
**结论**：reaEGC与St.57的dEGC转录组特征相关性最高，提示反应性EGC与发育EGC的分子相似性。

**关键数据**：相对于其他EGC类型，reaEGC的全局基因表达特征与St.57的dEGC最相似。

### Panel D and E — St.57 Cell Distribution and Marker Expression
**结论**：St.57背侧pallium中参与nptxEX发育的细胞呈现特征性标记基因表达（Nes、Nptx1）。

**关键数据**：Nes和Nptx1在St.57和15 DPI-4的空间分布相似。

### Panel F — Development Trajectory RNA Velocity
**结论**：RNA velocity流线图模拟发育过程中背侧pallium的预测轨迹。

**关键数据**：dEGC到dNBL到immature nptxEX到nptxEX的转换轨迹。

### Panel G and H — Development vs Regeneration Trajectory
**结论**：发育和再生过程中nptxEX生成的RNA velocity流线图显示平行转换轨迹：从EGC到IPC到未成熟和成熟神经元。

**关键数据**：St.57发育轨迹与15 DPI再生轨迹呈现相似模式。

### Panel I — Gene Expression Heatmap with Fuzzy Cluster
**结论**：发育和再生过程中nptxEX相关基因表达分为8个特征性组别，反映不同的动态变化模式。

**关键数据**：部分基因在发育和再生中共同上调或下调，部分呈现阶段特异性变化。

### Panel J — Gene Ontology Enrichment
**结论**：神经元分化、迁移、成熟、通讯和突触活动相关通路在两个过程中均上调；增殖、细胞周期和翻译起始相关通路在两个过程中均下调。

**关键数据**：染色质组织、TOR信号和TP53转录调控在再生转换中特异上调，提示代谢和基因组稳定性控制；自噬相关基因在再生中特异升高。

## 总体结论

本图系统比较了蝾螈脑室发育和再生过程中的神经发生模式。再生过程中nptxEX的生成重演了发育过程中nptxEX的生成模式，从EGC到IPC到未成熟和成熟神经元。reaEGC与dEGC转录组特征高度相似，暗示它们的功能相关性。分子 cascades 和潜在细胞谱系转换的相似性表明脑再生部分重演了发育过程，为理解脊椎动物脑再生的机制提供了重要见解。
