# Fig. 3 — Spatiotemporal transcriptomics of axolotl brain during regeneration

## Caption（原文）
> (A) Schematic diagram of sample collection (left). Overview of the sampled sections at homeostatic and regenerative stages (right).
> (B) Spatial visualization of cell types identified in the axolotl telencephalon sections by Stereo-seq at different stages of regeneration. IMN, immature neuron; obNBL, olfactory bulb neuroblast; reaEGC, reactive EGC; rIPC, regeneration intermediate progenitor cell; WSN, wound-stimulated neuron.
> (C) Line graph showing the fold change of the cell ratio in the injured hemisphere compared to the uninjured hemisphere across all stages. We set 4 as the maximum fold-change value for better visualization in the line plot.
> (D) Spatial visualization of marker gene expression in MCGs (left) and reaEGCs (right) on the 2 DPI section.
> (E) Spatial visualization of the expression level of Atf3 and the distribution of reaEGCs and WSNs on the sections of 2 DPI and 5 DPI.
> (F) UMAP visualization of the segmented cells from the dorsal regions of developmental stages (St.44, St.54, St.57) and regenerative stages (2 to 20 DPI), including nptxEX from both processes, immature nptxEX from developmental stages, and IMN and WSN from regenerative stages. Cells are colored according to cell type annotation (top). Bubble plot showing the expression levels of the specific markers of these four cell types (bottom).
> (G and H) Spatial visualization of the expression level of a ligand-receptor pair (Tnc and Sdc1) on the sections of 2, 5, and 20 DPI.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — Sample Collection Overview
**结论**：损伤后2、5、10、15、20、30、60天（DPI）采集蝾螈脑室截面，结合对照样本进行 Stereo-seq 分析，覆盖稳态和再生全阶段。

**关键数据**：损伤后60天细胞类型和分布完全恢复，表明形态学修复在30天内完成。

### Panel B — Cell Type Spatial Distribution During Regeneration
**结论**：鉴定出28种细胞类型，其中8种为再生特异类型；再生过程中细胞类型空间分布呈现动态变化。

**关键数据**：多截面整合分析显示同阶段细胞类型组成和基因表达谱高度一致。

### Panel C — Cell Population Dynamics Post-Injury
**结论**：损伤后两种细胞类型从2 DPI开始增加并维持到15 DPI：小胶质细胞（MCG，表达C1qb、Ctsl）和反应性EGC（reaEGC，表达Vim和S100a10）。

**关键数据**：MCG和reaEGC是损伤后主要响应的细胞类型。

### Panel D — MCG and reaEGC Marker Expression
**结论**：小胶质细胞和反应性EGC在损伤后2天呈现特异性标记基因表达，证实其损伤响应特性。

**关键数据**：MCG标记基因C1qb、Ctsl；reaEGC标记基因Vim、S100a10。

### Panel E — Atf3 Expression and WSN Distribution
**结论**：Atf3高表达的伤口刺激神经元（WSN）在2和5 DPI短暂诱导出现，其转录组特征不同于位置匹配的nptxEX，但类似于10-20 DPI出现的未成熟神经元（IMN）。

**关键数据**：WSN表达神经元生长相关基因（Dclk1、Gap43）上调，成熟神经元标记（Nptx1、Neurod6）下调。

### Panel F — UMAP of Dorsal Region Cells
**结论**：发育和再生阶段背侧区域细胞的UMAP显示WSN与nptxEX、IMN的转录组关系，揭示再生过程中细胞状态转换。

**关键数据**：WSN位于nptxEX和IMN之间，转录组特征支持WSN来源于局部nptxEX通过损伤诱导的转录组重塑。

### Panel G and H — Tnc-Sdc1 Ligand-Receptor Pair
**结论**：Tnc和Sdc1配体-受体对在伤口边缘的reaEGC和WSN邻近簇中强表达，提示reaEGC对损伤神经元的调控作用。

**关键数据**：TNC是成人神经龛中用于组织修复和再生的糖蛋白；SDC1也参与神经发生。

## 总体结论

本图展示了蝾螈脑室损伤后再生过程的空间转录组学特征。鉴定出28种细胞类型包括8种再生特异类型。MCG和reaEGC是损伤后主要响应的细胞类型，reaEGC可能来源于局部wntEGC和sfrpEGC。WSN可能由局部nptxEX通过转录组重塑转变而来，呈现去分化状态。Tnc-Sdc1配体-受体对在伤口边缘的强表达提示reaEGC与WSN之间的调控关系，参与协调再生响应。
