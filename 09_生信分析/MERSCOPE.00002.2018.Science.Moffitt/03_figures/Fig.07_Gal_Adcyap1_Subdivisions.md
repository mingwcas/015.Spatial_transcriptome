# Fig. 7 — Subdivisions of neuronal populations expressing Gal or Adcyap1

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 7. Subdivisions of neuronal populations expressing Gal or Adcyap1 revealed with MERFISH. (A) MERFISH subdivides galanin- and Adycap1-expressing cells into multiple transcriptionally and spatially distinct clusters. Color subdivision of the rectangles shows the nuclei spanned by individual clusters, colored identically to the nuclei abbreviations listed on the right. The nuclei abbreviations are as defined in Figs. 3F and 5B. (B) Expression distributions of selected marker genes and genes of interest for all neuronal clusters enriched in galanin (Gal). Expression distributions are calculated as in Fig. 2. (C) Spatial distributions of all inhibitory and excitatory Gal-enriched clusters. (D and E) As in (B) and (C) but for Adcyap1- and Bdnf-enriched clusters. The seven most enriched of the 14 Adcyap1- and Bdnf-enriched clusters are shown. (F) in situ hybridization images of cFos (red), Sncg (green), and overlay of an anterior slice of the preoptic region taken from a heat-stressed animal. The blue boxed region is magnified and shown on the right. Sncg is a marker for the scRNA-seq cluster e13 that corresponds to the MERFISH cluster E-3 (table S9).

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — Gal/Adcyap1 集群与核团范围
**结论**：同一传统 marker 覆盖多个转录和空间不同的细胞集群。

**关键数据**：Gal 细分为 10 个 MERFISH clusters；Adcyap1/Bdnf 识别 14 个 clusters。

### Panel B — Gal 集群表达谱
**结论**：Gal 集群拥有不同 marker、受体与神经调质程序。

**关键数据**：I-14:MPA/MPN/StHy 为跨多个核团的 Gal 集群，并在 parenting 中强烈激活。

### Panel C — Gal 集群空间分布
**结论**：Gal+ 细胞并非集中于单一 MPOA 区域，而是横跨多个核团。

**关键数据**：I-14、I-16、I-7、I-11 等集群具有不同空间范围，支持功能异质性。

### Panel D — Adcyap1/Bdnf 集群表达谱
**结论**：Adcyap1/Bdnf 作为 warm-sensitive marker 并不专一，覆盖多种分子集群。

**关键数据**：14 个集群中仅 7 个最富集者绘制；E-3 表达 Lepr、Prlr、Kiss1r 和 Tacr3。

### Panel E — Adcyap1/Bdnf 集群空间分布
**结论**：只有 E-3 的空间位置符合经典 warm-sensitive 细胞区域。

**关键数据**：E-3 位于 AvPe/Pe/VMPO/VLPO；其余 Adcyap1/Bdnf 集群分布在不同位置。

### Panel F — 热应激 cFos/Sncg 原位验证
**结论**：热应激诱导 E-3 区域的 cFos，且 Sncg 在 cFos+ 细胞中富集。

**关键数据**：Sncg 是 scRNA-seq e13 marker，e13 对应 MERFISH E-3；37°C warm challenge 后 4 小时成像。

## 总体结论
Fig. 7 把"Gal+"和"Adcyap1/Bdnf+"这类传统功能标签拆解为多个具有不同空间、受体和行为关联的细胞类型。E-3 是唯一同时符合 warm-sensitive 空间位置并在热应激中呈 cFos/Sncg 激活的 Adcyap1/Bdnf 集群，说明 MERFISH 能从宽泛 marker 中筛出真正功能相关的亚群。
