# Fig. 8 — Neuronal clusters activated during specific social behaviors

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 8. Neuronal clusters activated during specific social behaviors revealed with MERFISH. (A) Enrichment in cFos-positive cells within each neuronal cluster observed in males or females after displaying a given social behavior. Red bars marked with asterisks are clusters with statistically significant enrichment in cFos-positive cells, as compared with the fraction of cFos-positive cells in all cells (binomial test; false-discovery rate < 5%). Error bars represent standard error of the mean (n = 3 to 5 replicates). We measured fewer slices in behaviorally stimulated animals than in naïve animals (4 versus 12 slices per animal) (29), and only clusters in which at least 10 cells are present in two or more behavior replicates are depicted. (B) Expression distributions of selected marker genes and genes of interest for neuronal clusters enriched in cFos-positive cells in the tested social behaviors. Expression distributions are calculated as in Fig. 2. (C) Representative in situ hybridization images of 16-mm-thick sections from the preoptic region showing cFos expression in cells expressing markers of neuronal clusters activated during parenting, in virgin females, mothers, and fathers. Regions in blue dashed boxes are magnified and shown on the right. Red, green, and blue mark the listed genes, and white (or yellow for I-2) indicates coexpression in the merged images. Clusters that cannot be distinguished by a combination of two marker genes plus their spatial location (I-27 and I-10) were not tested. (D) Venn diagrams summarizing the clusters that were activated during specific behaviors in different sexes or physiological states.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — 各行为的 cFos+ 集群富集
**结论**：每种社会行为只显著富集少数神经元集群，且存在性别/生理状态差异。

**关键数据**：二项检验 FDR < 5%；n = 3–5 replicates；行为动物 4 slices/animal，naïve 12 slices/animal；显示门槛为 ≥10 cells 且至少 2 个重复。

### Panel B — 行为激活集群 marker
**结论**：激活集群具有可解释其行为关联的 Gal、Th、Esr1、Oxtr、Tac2 等分子特征。

**关键数据**：I-14 表达 Gal/Avpr1a；I-16 表达 Th、Slc18a2/Vmat2、Oprd1/Oprk1；I-15 表达 Esr1；I-10 表达 Oxtr。

### Panel C — ISH 验证
**结论**：双色/三色 ISH 在组织中验证了 cFos 与 cluster marker 的共表达。

**关键数据**：16 μm 厚切片；验证 I-14、E-1、I-2、I-16 等；I-27 与 I-10 因两个 marker + 空间仍无法区分而未测试。

### Panel D — 行为/状态 Venn diagram
**结论**：parenting、mating 与 aggression 共享部分集群，但也有状态特异的激活组合。

**关键数据**：parenting 共有核心 I-14；mother/father 额外 I-10；pup-directed aggression 为 I-16/I-24；inter-male aggression 为 I-16/I-2；female mating 以 I-15 为主，male mating 包括 I-2、I-11、I-14、I-16、I-33、E-8、E-15。

## 总体结论
Fig. 8 将分子细胞类型与社会行为功能对应起来：I-14（Gal/Moxd1）是所有 parenting 动物共享的核心集群；I-10（Oxtr）参与母亲和父亲育幼；I-15（Esr1）在雌性 mating 最突出；I-16（Gal/Th、推定多巴胺能）在雄性攻击及父亲育幼中激活，I-24 与 I-2 进一步区分对幼崽和成年雄性的攻击反应。结果支持由转录上不同的细胞类型组成行为回路，同时提示许多集群存在较弱的广泛共激活。
