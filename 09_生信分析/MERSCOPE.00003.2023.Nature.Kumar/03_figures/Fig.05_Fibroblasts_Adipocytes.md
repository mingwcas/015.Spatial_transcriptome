# Fig. 5 — Breast fibroblasts and adipocytes

## Caption（原文）
> a, Histopathological sections showing intralobular and interlobular fibroblasts. b, UMAP of 208,390 fibroblast cells showing 4 states. c, Top genes per fibroblast state. d, Collagen signature scores and FAP expression. e, Resolve data showing fibroblast genes in connective and intralobular areas. f, Histopathological section of adipose tissue. g, UMAP of 6,637 adipocytes from snRNA-seq. h, ST adipocyte cluster in P46. i, Top adipocyte, white and beige adipocyte markers in ST and snRNA.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a–d — 成纤维细胞
**结论：** 208,390 fibroblasts分为4状态，并具有胶原/FAP差异。
### Panel e — Resolve空间
**结论：** fibroblast marker在结缔与小叶内区域呈不同分布。
### Panel f–i — 脂肪细胞
**结论：** snRNA识别6,637 adipocytes，ST验证脂肪簇及白/米色脂肪标记。

## 总体结论
成纤维细胞具有区域化状态，snRNA与ST共同补足scRNA难以捕获的大型脂肪细胞。
