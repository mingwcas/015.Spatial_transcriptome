# Fig. 6 — Vascular, perivascular and lymphatic cells in the human breast

## Caption（原文）
> a, Histopathological section showing artery, vein and capillary. b, UMAP of 83,651 vascular endothelial cells showing 3 states. c, Canonical and top genes per vascular state. d, Histopathological section showing lymphatic duct. e, UMAP of 8,982 lymphatic endothelial cells showing 4 states. f, Canonical and top genes per lymphatic state. g, Resolve data showing vascular markers VWF, ACKR1, RBP7, GJA4 and lymphatic PROX1. h, CODEX showing vascular anti-CD31 and lymphatic anti-PDPN cells with basal anti-SMA. i, Histopathology of pericyte/capillary and artery/VSMC. j, UMAP of 52,638 perivascular cells showing 2 states. k, Markers and top genes per perivascular state.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a–c — 血管内皮
**结论：** 83,651 vascular cells分为arterial、venous和capillary三状态；占细胞11.7%、细胞核7.2%。
### Panel d–f — 淋巴内皮
**结论：** 8,982 lymphatic cells分为4状态；占细胞1.3%、细胞核3.5%。
### Panel g–h — 空间验证
**结论：** Resolve和CODEX分别以RNA和蛋白定位血管/淋巴细胞。
### Panel i–k — 周血管细胞
**结论：** 52,638 perivascular cells分为2状态，覆盖pericyte/VSMC谱系。

## 总体结论
乳腺血管、淋巴和周血管系统具有显著转录多样性并由多平台空间验证，为组织生态和疾病血管研究提供基线。
