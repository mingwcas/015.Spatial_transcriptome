# Fig. 2 — Spatial analysis of major breast cell types

## Caption（原文）
> a, ST experiment from patient P35 showing the H&E image with histopathological regions annotated and clustering results. b, Consensus heat map of the top four marker genes in each ST cluster from ten integrated tissue samples. c, Frequencies of ST clusters from ten tissue samples across four topographic regions. d, Resolve smFISH using a custom 100-gene panel, showing marker genes and cell segmentation. e, Spatial colocalization graph of cell types in smFISH data from 12 tissue samples. f, Cell type frequencies across 3 topographic regions from 12 Resolve tissues. g, CODEX data from P130 showing ductal–lobular structure with five protein markers and cell segmentation. h, Spatial colocalization graph in CODEX data from eight tissues. i, Cell type frequencies across three topographic regions from eight CODEX tissues. Scale bars, 1 mm (a) and 500 μm (d,g).

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a — Visium ST
**结论：** ST在病理标注区域显示空间簇。
**关键数据：** 10个整合组织样本；spot约55 μm。
### Panel b — ST markers
**结论：** 每个ST簇的top 4 marker与scRNA marker高度一致。
**关键数据：** 平均Pearson r=0.8。
### Panel c — ST区域频率
**结论：** adipose富集adipocyte，connective富集fibro/vascular，duct/lobule呈上皮差异。
### Panel d — Resolve分割
**结论：** 100基因panel实现单细胞原位标记。
### Panel e — Resolve邻域
**结论：** 上皮与B/T细胞共定位，fibro与vascular/lymphatic共定位。
### Panel f — Resolve区域频率
**结论：** duct富Basal/LumSec，lobule富Basal/LumHR，connective富fibro/vascular。
### Panel g — CODEX图像
**结论：** 34抗体蛋白成像覆盖大面积导管–叶结构。
### Panel h–i — CODEX邻域与频率
**结论：** 蛋白层邻域和区域细胞密度复现smFISH趋势。

## 总体结论
三种正交空间技术共同描绘乳腺细胞空间生态位，显示导管、叶和结缔/脂肪组织具有可重复的细胞组成与邻域结构。
