# Fig. 3 — Epithelial cells of the human breast

## Caption（原文）
> a, H&E section showing epithelial bilayer. b, scRNA UMAP of 240,804 epithelial cells showing three major types. c, snRNA UMAP of 55,557 epithelial nuclei. d, Keratin genes across types. e, 102,228 basal cells. f, 75,247 LumHR cells showing 3 states. g, 63,329 LumSec cells showing 7 states. h, Secretoglobin genes. i, HLA class I/II genes. j, Top genes per state. k, Lactation signature scores. l, G2/M scores. m, Proliferating fraction. n, CODEX PCNA in ducts/lobules. o, ST DE genes ducts versus lobules. p, Resolve genes specific to ductal/lobular regions. q, Resolve data in ducts/lobules.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a — 上皮双层
**结论：** 显示外层basal-myoepithelial与内层luminal结构。
### Panel b–d — 主类型与角蛋白
**结论：** 240,804 epithelial cells和55,557 nuclei归为Basal、LumHR、LumSec，并有类型特异角蛋白。
### Panel e–g — 状态UMAP
**结论：** LumHR 3 states；LumSec 7 states；Basal相对同质。
### Panel h–j — 状态marker
**结论：** LumSec-HLA、LumSec-KIT等显示免疫信号或谱系特征。
### Panel k–m — 功能/增殖
**结论：** lactation与G2/M评分及增殖比例揭示细胞状态差异。
### Panel n–q — 空间验证
**结论：** CODEX、ST、Resolve定位导管与叶的增殖和表达差异。

## 总体结论
乳腺上皮由三大类型和11种状态构成，LumSec最具异质性，且部分状态呈导管–叶空间偏好。
