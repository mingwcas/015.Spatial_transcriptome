# Fig. 4 — Immune cell ecosystem in human breast tissues

## Caption（原文）
> a, Immune and non-immune cell type frequencies in scRNA-seq and snRNA-seq. b, Frequencies by tissue source. c, Immune cell frequencies in CODEX (n=8) and Resolve smFISH (n=12). d, CODEX TDLU localization of six immune cell types/states. e, Resolve TDLU localization of three immune cell types. f, UMAP of 76,567 NK and T cells showing 14 states. g, Top genes for NK/T clusters. h, UMAP of 12,510 B cells showing five states. i, Top genes for B states. j, UMAP of 30,789 myeloid cells showing 15 cell types/states. k, Top genes for myeloid clusters. l–m, CODEX/Resolve localization. n, Frequency of immune cells proximal to vascular endothelial cells.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a–c — 频率
**结论：** 单细胞、细胞核及两种空间技术共同量化免疫细胞组成。
### Panel d–e — TDLU空间
**结论：** CODEX与Resolve显示免疫细胞在上皮、血管及TDLU中的定位。
### Panel f–g — NK/T
**结论：** 76,567 NK/T细胞解析14种状态及marker。
### Panel h–i — B细胞
**结论：** 12,510 B cells分为5种状态。
### Panel j–k — 髓系
**结论：** 30,789 myeloid细胞分为15种类型/状态。
### Panel l–n — 血管邻近
**结论：** 空间邻域量化免疫细胞与血管内皮的近邻关系。

## 总体结论
正常乳腺含丰富且多样的组织驻留免疫生态，T/NK、B和髓系谱系均有多状态，并呈现与TDLU和血管的空间组织。
