# Fig. 1 — Major cell types of the adult human breast

## Caption（原文）
> a, Anatomy of the adult human breast and a pathological H&E section, with illustrations of the major breast cell types. b, The workflow of the HBCA project. c, UMAP projection of scRNA-seq data from 714,331 cells integrated across 167 tissues from 126 women, showing 10 clusters that correspond to the major cell types. d, Consensus heat map of the top 7 genes expressed in each cell type cluster from averaged scRNA-seq data. e, UMAP representation of snRNA-seq data from 117,346 nuclei integrated across 24 tissues from 20 women, showing 11 cell type clusters. f, Consensus heat map of the top 7 genes expressed in each cell cluster from averaged snRNA-seq data.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a — 解剖与H&E
**结论：** 展示TDLU/叶、导管、结缔和脂肪四类空间区域及主要细胞。
**关键数据：** 四个主要组织区域。
### Panel b — HBCA workflow
**结论：** 220样本/132人经scRNA、snRNA、ST、smFISH和CODEX建立图谱。
**关键数据：** scRNA 126 women/167 tissues；snRNA 20 women/24 tissues。
### Panel c — scRNA UMAP
**结论：** 714,331细胞整合后识别10个主细胞簇。
**关键数据：** 167 tissues、126 women。
### Panel d — scRNA markers
**结论：** 每主类型top 7基因定义分子标志。
### Panel e — snRNA UMAP
**结论：** 117,346细胞核显示11簇并补充adipocyte/mast细胞。
### Panel f — snRNA markers
**结论：** 细胞核表达谱与细胞质RNA存在差异。

## 总体结论
Fig. 1建立成人正常乳腺多组学细胞图谱框架，兼顾细胞和细胞核测序以覆盖大体积脂肪细胞，并定义主细胞类型。
