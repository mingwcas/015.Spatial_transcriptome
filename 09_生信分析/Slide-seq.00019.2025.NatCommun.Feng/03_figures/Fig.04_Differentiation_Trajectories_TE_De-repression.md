# Fig. 4 — The prenatal Down syndrome brain exhibits altered differentiation trajectories and transposable element de-repression

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 4 | The prenatal Down syndrome brain exhibits altered differentiation trajectories and transposable element de-repression. a Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection (UMAP) visualization of RNA velocity trajectory analyses depicting developmental trajectories of cell types within mid-gestational euploid and Down syndrome (DS) brains from the single-nucleus (sn)RNA-seq dataset. Each arrow depicts the estimated direction and magnitude of change between cell states. b Latent time projection based on RNA velocity, capturing the relative progression of cells along pseudotemporal differentiation axes within euploid and DS brains. c Volcano plots of differential protein abundance from the proteomics analysis (n = 3 DS, n = 4 euploid). Each dot represents a protein and dots are colored according to enrichment: significantly upregulated (log2FC ≥0.25, FDR ≤0.05) in teal, significantly downregulated (log2FC ≤0.25, FDR ≤0.05) in purple, and non-significant genes in gray. d Barplot of normalized enrichment score (NES) for select gene ontology (GO) biological processes from the proteomics analysis. e Heatmap depicting the NES of transposable element (TE) subclasses in each cell subtype identified in the snRNA-seq dataset. f Immunostaining for LINE1-ORF1 (red) in SOX2+ (green) and DAPI-labeled nuclei (cyan) cells located near the ventricular zone (VZ) in prenatal human samples. g Bar plots displaying LINE1-ORF1 intensity in SOX2+ cells, measured in arbitrary units (arb. units). Each dot represents the average intensity for each prenatal human brain (n = 3 DS, n = 3 euploid). Statistical significance was assessed using a two-sided t-test; p-value = 0.022.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel a — RNA Velocity Trajectories
**结论**：DS脑中NPC向成熟神经元的分化轨迹减速
**关键数据**：DS NPC中RNA速度矢量的方向性和长度降低；新生儿浅层ExN中RNA速度向量消失

### Panel b — Latent Time
**结论**：DS脑中细胞沿分化轨迹的时间进程普遍延迟
**关键数据**：整倍体中latent time从NPC向新生儿神经元递增；DS中各细胞类型的latent time普遍降低

### Panel c — Proteomics Volcano
**结论**：DS产前脑中免疫相关蛋白广泛上调，突触和染色质相关蛋白下调
**关键数据**：884个蛋白差异丰度；66.7%上调，33.3%下调；补体蛋白（C1QA/B/C, C3, C4）和免疫球蛋白上调；GRM5/7、GRIK2等突触蛋白下调

### Panel d — Proteomics Pathway Enrichment
**结论**：DS脑中免疫激活伴随蛋白质翻译和染色质组织的下调
**关键数据**：Humoral immune response、Complement activation正富集；Synaptic signaling、Chromatin organization负富集

### Panel e — TE Subclass Heatmap
**结论**：DS脑中细胞类型特异性TE去抑制，特别是NPC中LINE1富集
**关键数据**：LINE1在oRG、vRG、CP、IP等NPC亚型以及ExN中正富集；转座酶在成熟ExN中富集；逆转座子在少突胶质细胞系中富集

### Panel f, g — LINE1-ORF1 Immunostaining
**结论**：LINE1-ORF1蛋白在DS SOX2+ NPC中显著增加
**关键数据**：p-value = 0.022 (n=3 DS, n=3 euploid)

## 总体结论
Fig. 4综合揭示了DS产前脑的三大发现：RNA速度分析显示分化轨迹改变；蛋白质组学显示免疫激活和突触/染色质下调；转座元件分析显示LINE1等TE在NPC中选择性去抑制，提示染色质调控异常。
