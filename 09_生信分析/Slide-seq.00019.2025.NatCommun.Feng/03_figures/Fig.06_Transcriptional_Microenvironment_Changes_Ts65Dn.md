# Fig. 6 — Down syndrome-associated transcriptional and local cellular architecture changes in trisomic mice

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 6 | Down syndrome-associated transcriptional and local cellular architecture changes in trisomic mice. a–c Volcano plots depicting differentially expressed genes (DEGs) in (a), neural stem cells (NSCs), b transit amplifying progenitors (TAPs), and c excitatory neuroblasts (ExNB) from the postnatal (P0) MERFISH subventricular zone (SVZ) dataset. Each dot represents a gene and dots are colored according to enrichment: significantly upregulated (log2FC > 0, FDR ≤ 0.05) in teal, significantly downregulated (log2FC < 0, FDR ≤0.05) in purple, and non-significant genes in gray. The horizontal line depicts FDR = 0.05, and the vertical line depicts log2FC = 0. d Microenvironment analysis of the P0 corpus callosum and spatial plots depicting increased proximity of microglia surrounding oligodendrocyte progenitor cells (OPCs) and decreased proximity of OPCs surrounding astrocytes in Ts65Dn relative to euploid. e Microenvironment analysis of the 6mo subventricular zone and spatial plots depicting increased proximity of astrocytes surrounding transit amplifying cells (TAPs) and OPCs in Ts65Dn relative to euploid.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel a — NSC DEGs
**结论**：Ts65Dn NSC中信号通路和神经发育基因异常
**关键数据**：WNT通路(Dvl1, Fzd7/8, Lrp5/6上调；Apc, Fzd5, Rac1下调)；NOTCH通路(Notch1/2/3上调)；Olig2(三体基因)上调；Fabp7/Slc1a3(星形胶质标记)下调

### Panel b — TAP DEGs
**结论**：Ts65Dn TAP中增殖和迁移基因下调
**关键数据**：Mki67/Egfr(增殖标记)下调；Lmnb1(核膜标记)下调；Unc5d/Dcx(迁移标记)下调；Ep300/Eif4g1(转录/翻译调控)下调

### Panel c — ExNB DEGs
**结论**：Ts65Dn兴奋性神经母细胞中发育调控基因改变
**关键数据**：Fto/Ythdc1/Ythdf1(表观转录组调控)上调；Ctnnb1/Mapk1改变；Dcx/Rac1下调

### Panel d — P0 Corpus Callosum Microenvironment
**结论**：Ts65Dn胼胝体中小胶质细胞与OPC空间关系改变
**关键数据**：Micro near OPC增加；OPC near Astro减少

### Panel e — 6mo SVZ Microenvironment
**结论**：6月龄Ts65Dn SVZ中星形胶质细胞与TAP/OPC空间关系改变
**关键数据**：Astro near TAP增加；Astro near OPC增加；提示星形胶质细胞在成年Ts65Dn脑中的异常行为

## 总体结论
Fig. 6揭示了Ts65Dn小鼠脑中NPC的信号通路失调和微环境改变，表明三体基因剂量效应影响神经发育和胶质细胞空间组织。
