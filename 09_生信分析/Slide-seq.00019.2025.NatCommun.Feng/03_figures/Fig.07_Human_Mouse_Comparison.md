# Fig. 7 — Cross-species comparison and discussion of conserved and divergent DS mechanisms

## Caption（原文）
> This figure summarizes the key findings and comparisons between human DS and Ts65Dn mouse models. The paper discusses overlapping patterns including dysregulation of post-transcriptional regulation, DNA repair and cell cycle genes, and divergent patterns including immune activation (prominent in human, limited in mouse) and TE de-repression (pronounced in human, less evident in mouse).

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### 论文讨论部分总结
**结论**：人鼠比较揭示了DS神经发育异常的保守和分化特征
**关键数据**：
- 保守特征：翻译调控(IF3F/Eif4g1)、表观转录组(YTHDF2/3/Ythdc1)、DNA修复和细胞周期调控
- 人特异性：广泛免疫激活(补体系统)
- 鼠特异性：6月龄时MX1 interferon response

## 总体结论
Fig. 7（讨论部分）总结了跨物种研究的重要发现，强调了小鼠模型在理解DS神经发育异常中的价值，同时也指出了Ts65Dn模型的局限性，为未来机制研究提供了重要方向。
