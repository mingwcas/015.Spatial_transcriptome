# Fig. 5 — Cellular and spatial landscape of postnatal and mature trisomic mouse brains

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 5 | Cellular and spatial landscape of postnatal and mature trisomic mouse brains. a Schematic overview of the MERFISH pipeline. b, c Spatial distribution of select cell type markers and segmentation of major anatomical regions on a representative b, postnatal (P0) and c 6 month (6mo) brain sample. d Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection (UMAP) representation of the P0 cortex. e Spatial distribution of cell subtypes on a cross-section of the P0 cortex. f UMAP representation of the P0 subventricular zone. g Spatial distribution of cell subtypes within the subventricular zone of the left hemisphere in the P0 brain. h UMAP representation of the P0 subventricular zone. UMAP representations of the 6mo i cortex, j subventricular zone, and k corpus callosum.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel a — MERFISH Pipeline Overview
**结论**：MERFISH可实现三体小鼠脑的高分辨率空间转录组分析
**关键数据**：500基因面板；P0 (n=3 Ts65Dn, n=3 euploid)；6mo (n=3 Ts65Dn, n=3 euploid)

### Panel b, c — Anatomical Region Segmentation
**结论**：MERFISH数据可准确划分脑解剖结构
**关键数据**：覆盖皮质浅层/深层、胼胝体、室管膜下区、尾状壳核等解剖区域

### Panel d — P0 Cortex UMAP
**结论**：P0皮层包含12个主要细胞类群
**关键数据**：ExN (Cux2, Mef2c, Slc17a6)、InN (Gad1/2)、Astro (Aqp4, Gfap, Slc1a3)、OPC (Pdgfra, Sox10, Olig1)、Vascular (Pdgfrb, Kcnj8, Pecam1)、Microglia (Cx3cr1, Tmem119)、Fibroblast (Fbln1)

### Panel e — P0 Cortex Spatial Distribution
**结论**：细胞类型空间分布与其解剖位置一致
**关键数据**：L2-3 ExN (Cux2)分布于浅层；L5-6 ExN (Tle4)分布于深层

### Panel f, g — P0 SVZ UMAP and Spatial
**结论**：SVZ包含NSC、TAP、神经母细胞等NPC群体
**关键数据**：NSC (Clu, Aldoc, Fabp7)、TAP (Mki67, Ascl1, Arx)、ExNB (Slc17a6, Dcx)

### Panel h — P0 Corpus Callosum
**结论**：胼胝体包含OPC、星形胶质细胞、血管和小胶质细胞
**关键数据**：8个主要类群

### Panel i-k — 6mo Brain Regions
**结论**：6月龄小鼠脑中细胞组成更加复杂和成熟
**关键数据**：皮层、SVZ和胼胝体中均显示更细化的细胞亚型

## 总体结论
Fig. 5建立了Ts65Dn三体小鼠脑在P0和6月龄两个时间点的空间细胞图谱，为理解DS发育和成熟过程中细胞和分子变化提供了重要资源。
