# Fig. 1 — Cellular characterization of the prenatal Down syndrome and euploid brain

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 1 | Cellular characterization of the prenatal Down syndrome and euploid brain. a Schematic overview of the multi-omics approach utilizing single-nucleus (sn)RNA-seq and Slide-seq. b Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection (UMAP) representation of all high-quality nuclei identified by snRNA-seq (n = 122,663). Each dot represents a nucleus. Cell type identity is delineated by the solid line. c Dotplot showing gene expression of canonical cell type markers used for cell type identification. Dot color represents normalized gene expression and dot diameter represents the proportion of nuclei expressing the gene. d–f UMAP representation of granular cell subtypes of d, neural progenitors, e, excitatory neurons, and f, interneurons. Nuclei are color-coded by their cell subtype identity. g Spatial representation of a representative Down syndrome (DS) brain and a magnified view of the ventricular zone, captured by Slide-seq. Each dot represents the cell type with the highest proportional representation on a given bead. Only neural progenitor cells (NPCs) are colored in the ventricular zone. h Spatial plots of genes used to delineate the ventricular zone and neocortex, including SOX2, VIM, MKI67, EOMES, DCX, MEF2C, SATB2, and TLE4. Dot color represents normalized gene expression from the 10th to 90th percentiles.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel a — Experimental Design Overview
**结论**：多组学整合策略是研究DS产前脑的有效方法
**关键数据**：整合snRNA-seq（n=5 DS, n=5整倍体）和Slide-seq（n=3 DS, n=3整倍体），覆盖13-19孕周

### Panel b — snRNA-seq UMAP
**结论**：DS和整倍体产前脑共享相似的整体细胞类型构成
**关键数据**：122,663个高质量细胞核，包含NPC、兴奋性神经元、抑制性神经元、星形胶质细胞前体、少突胶质细胞前体、小胶质细胞和血管细胞

### Panel c — Cell Type Markers
**结论**：细胞类型可被经典标记基因准确区分
**关键数据**：NPC表达SOX2/PAX6/EOMES；兴奋性神经元表达MEF2C/TLE4；抑制性神经元表达GAD1/PROX1

### Panel d — NPC Subtypes
**结论**：NPC包含5个细分类：oRG、vRG、CP、IP（兴奋型）和IP IN（抑制型）
**关键数据**：oRG表达TNC/LIFR/HOPX；vRG表达CRYAB/FBXO3

### Panel e — Excitatory Neuron Subtypes
**结论**：兴奋性神经元包含6个细分类，按皮质层分布
**关键数据**：包括L2-4 ExN (MEF2C/RORB)、L5-6 ExN (TLE4)、NB L2-3 ExN (CUX2/SLC17A6)等

### Panel f — Interneuron Subtypes
**结论**：抑制性神经元包含多个细分类，包括皮层和纹状体来源
**关键数据**：InN 1-4为皮层抑制性神经元；InN M1/M2为中等棘神经元(MSN)

### Panel g — Slide-seq Spatial Mapping
**结论**：Slide-seq成功解析DS脑的空间细胞分布
**关键数据**：空间映射显示心室区（n=14,490）和新皮层（n=159,508）的细胞类型注释与其空间位置一致

### Panel h — Spatial Gene Expression
**结论**：关键发育基因的空间表达模式与预期解剖位置相符
**关键数据**：SOX2/VIM/MKI67富集于心室区；MEF2C/SATB2/TLE4富集于新皮层

## 总体结论
Fig. 1建立了DS和整倍体产前人脑的细胞类型和空间转录组参考图谱，揭示了细胞类型的整体构成在DS和整倍体之间相似，但空间分布和细分类的表达模式存在差异。
