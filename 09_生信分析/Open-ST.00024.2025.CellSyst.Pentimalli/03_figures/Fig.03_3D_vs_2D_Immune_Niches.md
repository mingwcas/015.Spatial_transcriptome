# Fig. 3 — 3D neighborhoods improve the identiﬁcation and spatial mapping of immune niches

## Caption（原文）
> (A) Design of 2D and 3D cellular neighborhoods. Cells in a 50 μm-radius circle around the center cell compose its 2D neighborhood (blue), while cells in a 40 μm-radius circle in the sections above and below are unique to its 3D neighborhood (orange).
> (B) 3D neighborhoods are richer than their 2D counterparts. Distributions of the total number of neighbors (left), unique cell types (middle), and alpha diversity (right) for each cellular neighborhood in 2D (blue) and 3D (orange). ****: t test p values < 0.005, n = 218,378.
> (C) Dendritic cell niches are only identiﬁed in 3D. UMAP of 2D cellular neighborhoods colored by 2D multicellular niche assignments.
> (D) T cell niches have the lowest 2D-3D concordance. Alluvial plot of multicellular niche assignments. Lines follow the same cell in 3D (left) and 2D (right).
> (E) 3D neighborhoods capture the spatial continuity of T cell niches. Blue: 3D surface rendering of 'T cell niches,' red: cells assigned to 'T cell niches' in 2D, gray: 'tumor core and surface.'
> (F) 3D neighborhoods reveal the 3D spatial relationships between multicellular niches in the TME. 3D rendering reveals how the 'tumor surface' (red) covers the 'tumor core' (yellow). 'Dendritic cell niches' (purple) and 'macrophage niches' (pink) are embedded in the tumor surface, while 'T cell niches' (blue) are found mainly outside of the tumor bed.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — 2D/3D邻域设计
**结论**：2D邻域为50μm半径的圆形区域，3D邻域额外包含上下切片各40μm半径的邻域，面积大2.28倍。

**关键数据**：2D面积7854μm²，3D额外面积2×5027μm²。

### Panel B — 3D邻域更丰富
**结论**：3D邻域包含更多邻居（中位71 vs 32）、更多细胞类型（9 vs 7）和更高的alpha多样性（Chao指数10.5 vs 8），差异均显著（p<0.005）。

**关键数据**：n=218,378个邻域；所有差异p<0.005。

### Panel C — 树突状细胞niche仅在3D中鉴定
**结论**：2D分析无法识别树突状细胞niche，该niche仅在3D邻域分析中被发现，并经IF验证。

**关键数据**：2D中缺少dendritic cell niche；2D分析中有"macrophage-rich stroma"（仅2D特有）。

### Panel D — T细胞niche一致性最低
**结论**：T细胞niche在2D和3D间的一致性最低（46.2%），35.3%的T细胞niche细胞在2D中被错误分配到desmoplastic stroma。

**关键数据**：总一致性64.1%；T细胞niche一致性46.2%；T细胞niche大小3D: 32,272 cells vs 2D: 17,355 cells。

### Panel E — T细胞niche空间连续性
**结论**：3D分析恢复了T细胞niche的空间连续性，3D特有细胞环绕并连接2D中看似不连续的T细胞niche区域。

**关键数据**：3D渲染显示T细胞niche的连续结构。

### Panel F — Niche空间嵌套关系
**结论**：3D渲染揭示了niche间的空间嵌套关系：肿瘤表面包裹肿瘤核心，树突状细胞和巨噬细胞niche嵌入肿瘤表面，T细胞niche主要位于肿瘤bed外部。

**关键数据**：3D空间关系可视化。

## 总体结论
Fig. 3系统证明了3D邻域分析相比2D的优势：3D分析捕获更丰富的细胞多样性，恢复免疫niche的空间连续性，并揭示niche间的三维空间关系。特别是树突状细胞niche的3D特异性鉴定具有重要的免疫学意义。
