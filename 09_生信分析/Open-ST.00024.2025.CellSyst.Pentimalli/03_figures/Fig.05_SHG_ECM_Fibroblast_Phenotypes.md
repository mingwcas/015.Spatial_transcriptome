# Fig. 5 — Second harmonic imaging links ECM compartments with ﬁbroblast phenotypes in situ

## Caption（原文）
> (A) Second harmonic generation (SHG) imaging captures extracellular matrix (ECM) remodeling in the TME. Collagen (green) and elastin (magenta) ﬁbers were quantiﬁed by SHG in section 3.
> (B) SHG enables the unbiased assignment of cells to speciﬁc ECM compartments. Quantiﬁcation of elastin (x axis) and collagen (y axis) ﬁbers in cellular neighborhoods highlights the transition from an elastin-rich, collagen-poor homeostatic ECM to a collagen-rich, elastin-poor desmoplastic ECM. Cells are colored by k-means cluster assignment (k = 3).
> (C) SHG spatially maps ECM compartments in the TME. Cells in section 4 are colored by their ECM cluster assignments.
> (D) Fibroblasts feature heterogeneous transcriptomic phenotypes in the TME. UMAP of ﬁbroblast gene expression colored by transcriptomic clusters.
> (E) Fibroblast phenotypes are spatially organized in the TME. Spatial distribution of ﬁbroblast transcriptomic clusters (section 4).
> (F) Fibroblast phenotypes are linked with ECM remodeling. Boxplot of collagen and elastin ﬁber abundance in ﬁbroblast neighborhoods across ﬁbroblast clusters.
> (G–I) Fibroblast expression of ECM regulators is spatially organized within ECM compartments. 3D volumetric rendering of TIMP1 (G), IGF1 (H), and INHBA (I) ﬁbroblast expression density.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — SHG成像
**结论**：SHG成像捕获了TME中ECM的异质性，胶原（绿）和弹性蛋白（品红）的空间分布与主要组织结构一致。

**关键数据**：section 3的SHG图像。

### Panel B — ECM区室鉴定
**结论**：基于细胞邻域的弹性蛋白和胶原含量，k-means聚类（k=3）将细胞分为3个ECM区室：稳态ECM（弹性蛋白丰富）、降解ECM（两者均少）和促结缔组织增生ECM（胶原丰富）。

**关键数据**：k=3，从弹性蛋白丰富/胶原少到弹性蛋白少/胶原丰富的连续转变。

### Panel C — ECM区室空间映射
**结论**：ECM区室在空间上清晰分离，与SHG原图和多细胞niche分布一致。

**关键数据**：section 4的ECM区室空间分布。

### Panel D-F — 成纤维细胞表型与ECM关联
**结论**：6种成纤维细胞转录组状态与特定ECM区室紧密对应：肌成纤维细胞在降解ECM（最低胶原信号），活化成纤维细胞在促结缔组织增生ECM（最高胶原信号）。

**关键数据**：成纤维细胞是TME中最丰富的细胞类型；44.5%在desmoplastic ECM，32.8%在degraded，22.7%在homeostatic。

### Panel G-I — ECM调控因子空间分布
**结论**：ECM调控因子在空间上受限于特定ECM区室：TIMP1在稳态ECM，IGF1在促结缔组织增生ECM，INHBA在降解ECM。

**关键数据**：3D体积渲染显示基因表达密度的空间分布。

## 总体结论
Fig. 5将SHG ECM成像与空间转录组整合，揭示了TME中ECM从稳态到降解再到促结缔组织增生的连续重塑过程。成纤维细胞的功能特化与ECM区室紧密关联，鉴定出关键的ECM组成因子（FN1, COL11A1）和调控因子（TIMP1, IGF1, INHBA）。
