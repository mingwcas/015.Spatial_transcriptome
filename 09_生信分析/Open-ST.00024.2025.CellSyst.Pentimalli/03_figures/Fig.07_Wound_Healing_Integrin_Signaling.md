# Fig. 7 — A wound healing-like, multicellular program activates integrin signaling in the EMT niche

## Caption（原文）
> (A) LGALS1 and NDRG1 expression characterizes tumor cells in the EMT niche. Scatter plot of differential gene expression in tumor cells inside vs. outside the EMT niche. x axis: difference in the percentage of expressing tumor cells. y axis: log2 fold changes of the average SCT-normalized tumor gene expression.
> (B and C) NDRG1 (marker of brain metastasis) and LGALS1 (secreted during wound healing) expression is restricted to the tumor cells in the EMT niche (black box). 3D surface rendering of the gene expression density of EMT niche tumor marker genes.
> (D) Myoﬁbroblasts accumulate in the EMT niche. 3D spatial density plot of myoﬁbroblasts.
> (E and F) VEGFA (secreted in response to hypoxia and wound healing) and IGFBP5 (local regulator of IGF signaling) expression is restricted to ﬁbroblasts in the EMT niche (black box). 3D surface rendering of the gene expression density of EMT niche ﬁbroblast marker genes.
> (G) SPP1 (secreted during wound healing) expression is restricted to macrophages in the EMT niche (black box). 3D surface rendering of the gene expression density of EMT niche macrophage marker gene.
> (H) Tumor integrin signaling distinguishes the EMT from the rest of the tumor bed. 3D volumetric rendering of integrin signaling density, computed as the sum of all EMT niche-enriched interactions received by all tumor cells.
> (I) A wound healing-like multicellular program characterizes the EMT niche and converges on the activation of integrin signaling. Summary scheme of selected cellular and molecular interactions in the EMT niche, revealing mechanism-based personalized targets for cancer interception. The pill symbol indicates druggable interactions.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — EMT niche差异表达基因
**结论**：NDRG1和LGALS1是EMT niche肿瘤细胞中差异表达最强的基因。

**关键数据**：散点图显示EMT niche内外肿瘤细胞的差异表达。

### Panel B-C — EMT niche标志物3D渲染
**结论**：NDRG1（脑转移标志物）和LGALS1（伤口愈合开关）的表达几乎完全限于EMT niche中的肿瘤细胞。

**关键数据**：该患者在诊断后11个月发生脑转移，与NDRG1富集一致。

### Panel D — 肌成纤维细胞聚集
**结论**：肌成纤维细胞在EMT niche中的密度峰值高于肿瘤bed其余部分。

**关键数据**：3D空间密度图显示myofibroblast在EMT niche的局部富集。

### Panel E-F — 成纤维细胞标志物
**结论**：VEGFA（低氧/伤口愈合响应因子）和IGFBP5（IGF信号调控因子）的表达限于EMT niche中的成纤维细胞。

**关键数据**：3D表面渲染显示基因表达密度的空间受限分布。

### Panel G — 巨噬细胞标志物
**结论**：SPP1（伤口愈合中分泌的骨桥蛋白）的表达限于EMT niche中的巨噬细胞。

**关键数据**：SPP1+巨噬细胞具有M2和组织修复表型（GLUL+, GPNMB+）。

### Panel H — 整合素信号3D渲染
**结论**：整合素信号密度（所有EMT niche富集相互作用的总和）精确标记了EMT niche，与肿瘤bed其余部分明显区分。

**关键数据**：3D体积渲染显示整合素信号的空间分布。

### Panel I — 伤口愈合样通讯网络示意图
**结论**：EMT niche中存在伤口愈合样多细胞程序：肿瘤细胞（LGALS1, IGF2, THBS1）、成纤维细胞（VEGFA, IGFBP5, FN1, THBS2, COL1A2, COL6A3）和巨噬细胞（SPP1）的配体汇聚于肿瘤整合素受体（ITGB1, ITGB4, ITGB6）。药丸符号标记可靶向的相互作用。

**关键数据**：10条富集的受体-配体相互作用全部汇聚于整合素信号。

## 总体结论
Fig. 7揭示了EMT niche的分子驱动机制：一个伤口愈合样多细胞程序激活肿瘤整合素信号。肿瘤细胞LGALS1驱动肌成纤维细胞活化和M2巨噬细胞极化，成纤维细胞VEGFA/IGFBP5和巨噬细胞SPP1的配体汇聚于肿瘤整合素受体，提供了一个整合的促侵袭信号。该发现为联合治疗（免疫检查点抑制剂+FAK/整合素信号抑制剂）提供了分子基础。
