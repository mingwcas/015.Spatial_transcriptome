# Fig. 3 — Typical library-construction results

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 3 | Typical results seen during the library construction steps. All data ... mouse brain. a, Bioanalyzer after IVT: (1) successful, aRNA average >200 nt and high yield; (2) correct length but lower yield; (3) correct length but very low yield; (4) adequate yield but short average length. b, qPCR curves: same tissue sections normally vary 0–1 cycle; a late sample 2–3 cycles indicates less successful library; biological samples may differ ~5 cycles. c, finished libraries: successful average length ~400–500 bp; short libraries predict reduced genome mapability; low yield may require 1–2 more PCR cycles. FU, fluorescence units.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a — aRNA Bioanalyzer
**结论**：IVT产物长度和产量是早期文库QC。
**关键数据**：理想aRNA平均长度>200 nt；固定/渗透不佳可造成短峰。
### Panel b — qPCR
**结论**：qPCR曲线用于比较重复并确定index PCR循环。
**关键数据**：同样本成功重复相差0–1 cycle；异常样本晚2–3 cycles；样本间可差约5 cycles。
### Panel c — 成品文库
**结论**：成品应有约400–500 bp平均长度和足够产量。
**关键数据**：低产或过短分别提示增加PCR循环或降低可比对率。

## 总体结论
多阶段QC把aRNA质量、扩增循环和最终文库长度连接起来，是保证空间测序数据质量的关键。
