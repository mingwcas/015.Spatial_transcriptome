# Fig. 7 — Detected genes and unique transcripts

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 7 | Number and spatial distribution of detected genes and unique transcripts from typical experiments. a (Top), genes per spot in typical mouse olfactory bulb and breast cancer experiments; fewer genes are detected in clinical samples (~1,500 versus ~4,000). (Bottom), spatial distribution; lowest numbers occur in regions with few cells or fibrous tissue. b (Top), unique transcripts per spot; clinical samples usually have fewer (~3,000 versus ~14,000). (Bottom), spatial distribution shows the same dependence on cell number and fibrous tissue. Scale bars, 500 μm.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a — genes per spot及空间分布
**结论**：小鼠嗅球spot检测基因多于乳腺癌，且低细胞/纤维区最低。
**关键数据**：均值约1,518（乳腺癌）与4,038（小鼠嗅球）；正文概括约1,500 vs ~4,000。
### Panel b — unique transcripts
**结论**：unique molecule检测同样受样本类型和细胞密度影响。
**关键数据**：图中均值约3,055（乳腺癌）与14,144（小鼠嗅球）；正文概括约3,000 vs ~14,000。

## 总体结论
在约80–100 million reads/sample的起始深度下，Spatial Transcriptomics可获得spot级基因和UMI分子量化，但检测量强烈依赖组织细胞数、纤维成分和样本质量。
