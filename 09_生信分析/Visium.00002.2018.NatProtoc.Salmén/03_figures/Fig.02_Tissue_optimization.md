# Fig. 2 — Tissue optimization

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 2 | Bright-field and fluorescence images demonstrating the tissue-optimization stage on mouse olfactory bulb. a, A slide with an even smear of probes is used rather than one with spatial spots. Tissue is treated as for library preparation, but fluorescent nucleotides are introduced during cDNA synthesis. (1) Bright-field image ... (2) fluorescently labeled cDNA on the surface after tissue removal ... b, Slide areas after different extents of permeabilization ... (1) underpermeabilized ... (2) accurate ... (3) overpermeabilized ... Scale bars, 500 μm (a, top); 100 μm (a, bottom; b).

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a — 明场与cDNA footprint
**结论**：荧光cDNA印迹应跟随原组织细胞分布。
**关键数据**：组织去除后以Cy3标记cDNA；无细胞区域信号低。
### Panel b — 通透化梯度
**结论**：中等通透化同时保留强信号和清晰边界。
**关键数据**：不足导致细胞未产生完整footprint；过度导致信号弱且扩散模糊。

## 总体结论
Cy3 footprint提供无需测序的组织特异性参数选择标准，降低捕获不足或空间扩散。
