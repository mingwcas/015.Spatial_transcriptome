# Fig. 4 — Experimental flowchart

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 4 | Flowchart of the experimental part of the Spatial Transcriptomics approach. Day 1 includes cryosectioning, fixation, staining, pre-permeabilization imaging, permeabilization and cDNA synthesis overnight. Day 2 starts with tissue removal and release of cDNA ... second-strand synthesis and IVT overnight. During days 3–4, libraries are finished. Sequencing is usually carried out on day 5 or 6. Possible pause points and recommended storage temperatures are marked.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a — Day 1
**结论**：完成组织上阵列、染色、成像、通透化和过夜RT。
**关键数据**：Steps 1–31；RT 6–15 h。
### Panel b — Day 2
**结论**：去组织、释放cDNA、二链合成和IVT建立扩增产物。
**关键数据**：Steps 32–74；IVT过夜。
### Panel c — Days 3–6
**结论**：接头、RT、qPCR、index PCR、纯化、测序及计算处理依次完成。
**关键数据**：测序通常第5–6天；数据处理4–21 h。

## 总体结论
流程图将约5–6天的实验和测序工作拆分为可暂停节点，强调组织处理、文库制备和计算阶段的连续性。
