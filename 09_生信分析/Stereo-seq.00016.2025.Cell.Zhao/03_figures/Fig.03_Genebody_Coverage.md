# Fig. 3 — Stereo-seq V2 conveys whole-gene-body coverage in total RNA profiling

## Caption（原文）
> (A) Gene body coverage comparison of Stereo-seq V1, V2, Visium, and STRS. Only reads aligned to the exons were counted.
> (B) Comparison of reads mapped to Gapdh and Cx3cl1 genome regions of different ST techniques. Arrows indicate the transcription direction from 5′ to 3′. Wide blocks represent the exons, and narrow blocks represent for UTRs.
> (C) Left: the pie chart shows the proportion of genes commonly and uniquely captured by Stereo-seq V1 and V2. Right: the pie chart shows the composition of gene types specifically captured by Stereo-seq V2.
> (D) Grouped bar plot shows a comparison of the different ncRNA types detected by the different techniques. Data are represented as mean ± 95% CI.
> (E) Comparison of reads aligned to the snoRNAs Gm22980, Snord38a, Gm22330, and Snord55 on the antisense strand of Rps8 between different techniques.
> (F) Heatmap showing the differentially expressed ncRNAs across coronal mouse hemibrain sections profiled by Stereo-seq V2.
> (G) Spatial visualization of the genes highlighted in red in (F).
> See also Figure S3.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — Gene Body Coverage
**结论**：V2显示均匀的基因体覆盖，而V1、Visium和STRS均呈现3'偏倚。

**关键数据**：V2的覆盖曲线在基因体上保持平坦。

### Panel B — Gapdh and Cx3cl1 Coverage
**结论**：仅V2在所有外显子上显示均匀覆盖。

**关键数据**：V2的exon coverage更均匀。

### Panel C — Gene Capture Comparison
**结论**：V2独有检测到14,643个基因(32.7%)，包括3,895个ncRNA(26.6%)。

**关键数据**：V1和V2共有29,416个基因(65.7%)；V1独有1,714个(1.6%，多为假基因)。

### Panel D — ncRNA Detection
**结论**：V2在所有ncRNA类型上的检测显著优于其他技术。

**关键数据**：snoRNA、snRNA、lncRNA等各类ncRNA检测数量均最高。

### Panel E — snoRNA Detection
**结论**：V2能检测到反义链上的snoRNA，而其他技术无法检测。

**关键数据**：Gm22980、Snord38a、Gm22330、Snord55在V2中特异检测到。

### Panel F and G — ncRNA Spatial Distribution
**结论**：ncRNA在大脑不同区域呈现特异性的空间分布模式。

**关键数据**：Gm49678在cortical amygdalar和piriform-amygdalar高表达；Rmst和Sox2ot在多个区域高表达。

## 总体结论
随机引物捕获策略使V2实现全基因体均匀覆盖，显著提升了non-poly(A) RNA（包括ncRNA）的检测能力。
