# Fig. 6 — Stereo-seq V2 captures host and Mtb RNAs simultaneously

## Caption（原文）
> (A) Summary of mouse Mtb infection model.
> (B) H&E staining of the mouse lung at different times PI.
> (C) The magnified images showing the acid-fast staining of Mtb in the regions squared in (B).
> (D) Spatial visualization of marker genes and Mtb RNA Rvm01 and Rvm02 expression at different times PI. WT represents uninfected wild-type mouse. 1 day, 4 weeks, and 8 weeks represent 1 day, 4 weeks, and 8 weeks PI, respectively.
> (E) Dot plot showing the expression of Rvm01, Rvm02, and total Mtb RNAs at different times PI.
> (F) Bar plot showing the CFU counting results at different times PI (1 day, n = 4; 4 weeks, n = 10; 8 weeks, n = 10; p value is determined using t test). Data are represented as mean ± 95% CI.
> (G) Spatial visualization of the weighted expression score for genes in the Mtb RNA-related modules (module 3 at 4 weeks PI; module 8 at 8 weeks PI).
> (H) Venn plot showing the overlapping of genes involved in Mtb RNA-related modules.
> (I) Bar plot showing the enrichment p value on top enriched GO term gene set for genes of different parts from (H). The enrichment analysis was performed using Metascape.
> (J) Heatmap showing the expression of BCR heavy-chain subclasses at different times PI.
> (K) Spatial visualization of Ighm, Ighg2c, and Igha at different times PI.
> See also Figure S5.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — Infection Model
**结论**：Mtb感染小鼠模型时间线：感染后1天、4周、8周取样。

**关键数据**：1天、4周、8周PI三个时间点。

### Panel B and C — Histopathology
**结论**：H&E和抗酸染色显示感染随时间动态变化，4周最严重。

**关键数据**：4周：多细菌感染；8周：免疫浸润增加，细菌减少。

### Panel D and E — Mtb RNA Dynamics
**结论**：Mtb RNA在4周达峰，8周下降，与CFU一致。

**关键数据**：Rvm01、Rvm02表达动态；4周PI峰值。

### Panel F — CFU Validation
**结论**：CFU计数与Mtb RNA表达一致。

**关键数据**：1天、4周(n=10)、8周(n=10)的CFU数据。

### Panel G and H — Gene Modules
**结论**：鉴定4周和8周特异的Mtb相关基因模块。

**关键数据**：4周module 3，8周module 8；炎症反应、细胞凋亡、铁死亡通路富集。

### Panel I — GO Enrichment
**结论**：共同基因富集于感染相关通路；8周特异基因富集于适应性免疫响应。

**关键数据**：Common: 炎症反应、凋亡、铁死亡；8 weeks specific: 适应性免疫响应。

### Panel J and K — BCR Expression
**结论**：BCR重链表达在8周增加，Ighe几乎不表达。

**关键数据**：Ighm、Ighg2c、Igha在8周高表达。

## 总体结论
V2首次实现宿主-病原体转录组的同步空间分析，揭示了Mtb感染动态和免疫应答。
