# Fig. 5 — Stereo-seq V2 identifies tumor-associated AS events

## Caption（原文）
> (A) Illustration of the five major types of AS events.
> (B) Bar plot showing the number of the five AS events supported by different splice-junction-spanning reads (UMI counts).
> (C) Volcano plot displaying differential AS events between tumor and normal regions. Positive and negative "Inclevel difference" values indicate upregulation and downregulation in the normal region, respectively.
> (D) Bar plot presenting the number of AS events up- and downregulated in tumor regions.
> (E) Heatmap showing the expression of representative gene AS events in different regions. All data were normalized using Z score normalization across regions.
> (F and G) Spatial mapping (F) and sashimi plot (G) of two GIPC1 isoforms.
> See also Figure S4.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — AS Types
**结论**：鉴定5种主要AS类型：SE(跳过外显子)、MXE(互斥外显子)、A3SS(3'可变剪接位点)、A5SS(5'可变剪接位点)、RI(内含子保留)。

**关键数据**：5种AS类型示意图。

### Panel B — AS Event Counts
**结论**：SE事件最丰富，其次是A3SS和A5SS。

**关键数据**：SE: 964, MXE: 56, A3SS: 191, A5SS: 142, RI: 139；平均支持UMI = 19。

### Panel C and D — Differential AS
**结论**：肿瘤与正常区域间存在显著的差异AS事件。

**关键数据**：FDR ≤ 0.05筛选；上调和下调AS事件数量相当。

### Panel E — Heatmap
**结论**：多种基因在肿瘤区域显示差异AS事件。

**关键数据**：ZNF226显示内含子保留；GIPC1显示外显子跳跃。

### Panel F and G — GIPC1 Isoforms
**结论**：GIPC1的SE事件在肿瘤区域显著上调。

**关键数据**：GIPC1是乳腺癌生物标志物。

## 总体结论
全基因体覆盖使V2能够分析空间分辨率的可变剪接事件，揭示肿瘤相关的AS变化。
