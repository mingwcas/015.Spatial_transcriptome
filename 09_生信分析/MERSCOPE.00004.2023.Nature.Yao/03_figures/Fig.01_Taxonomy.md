# Fig. 1 — Transcriptomic cell-type taxonomy of the whole mouse brain

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 1 | Transcriptomic cell-type taxonomy of the whole mouse brain. a, Left, the transcriptomic taxonomy tree of 338 subclasses organized in a dendrogram (10xv2: n=1,699,939 cells; 10xv3: n=2,341,350 cells; 10x Multiome: n=1,687 nuclei)... b–e, UMAP representation of all cell types coloured by class, subclass, brain region and major neurotransmitter type.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a — taxonomy tree
**结论**：四级层级组织全脑细胞类型。
**关键数据**：34 classes、338 subclasses、1,201 supertypes、5,322 clusters；scRNA/Multiome细胞数如caption。
### Panel b — class UMAP
**结论**：类别在转录空间形成分离结构。
### Panel c — subclass UMAP
**结论**：展示细粒度亚类关系。
### Panel d — region UMAP
**结论**：转录类型与脑区对应。
### Panel e — neurotransmitter UMAP
**结论**：递质标签跨层级分布。

## 总体结论
Fig.1建立全脑转录组taxonomy，并以MERFISH空间数据和多种颜色编码展示类别、区域及递质组织。
