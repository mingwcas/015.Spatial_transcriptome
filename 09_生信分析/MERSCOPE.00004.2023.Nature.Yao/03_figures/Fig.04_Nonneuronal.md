# Fig. 4 — Non-neuronal cell types and immature neuronal types

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 4 | Non-neuronal cell types and immature neuronal types. a, UMAP representation of the NN–IMN–GC neighbourhood coloured by subclass. b–d, three subpopulations indicated in a are highlighted: astrocytes, ependymal cells and VLMCs. UMAP representation and representative MERFISH sections are coloured and numbered by cluster. e–f, colocalization of tanycyte, ependymal, VLMC and CHOR clusters around ventricles.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a — NN–IMN–GC UMAP
**结论**：展示非神经元、未成熟神经元和颗粒细胞邻域。
### Panels b–d — astrocyte/ependymal/VLMC
**结论**：UMAP与MERFISH共同解析三类细胞。
### Panel e — V3/ME
**结论**：tanycyte、ependymal、VLMC在脑室周围共定位。
### Panel f — ventricles
**结论**：VLMC、CHOR与ependymal在多脑室共定位。

## 总体结论
非神经元及未成熟神经元具有清晰的转录分类与脑室相关空间生态位。
