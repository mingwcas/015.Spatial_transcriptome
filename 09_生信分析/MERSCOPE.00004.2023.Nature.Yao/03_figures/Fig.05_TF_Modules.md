# Fig. 5 — Transcription factor modules across the whole mouse brain

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 5 | Transcription factor modules across the whole mouse brain. a, Distribution of the number of differentially expressed TFs between neuronal/non-neuronal classes, classes, subclasses and within subclasses. b, Cross-validation accuracy using all 8,460 markers, 534 TF markers, functional, adhesion or random markers. c, Density plot of marker-gene correlations. d, Expression of key TFs for each subclass organized in TF co-expression modules.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a — DEG数量
**结论**：TF差异随分类层级变化并可区分细胞群。
### Panel b — 交叉验证
**结论**：534 TF subclass recall 0.94，接近全部DEG 0.98。
### Panel c — 相关性密度
**结论**：TF marker在簇间相关性最低，区分力最强。
### Panel d — TF热图/模块
**结论**：52个模块对应特定细胞群；示例包括Tbr1/Satb2和Dlx家族。

## 总体结论
转录因子组合表达是跨全脑taxonomy定义细胞身份的关键分子基础。
