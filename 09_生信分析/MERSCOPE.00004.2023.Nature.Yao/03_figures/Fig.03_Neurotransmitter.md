# Fig. 3 — Modulatory neurotransmitter types and their distribution throughout the brain

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 3 | Modulatory neurotransmitter types and their distribution throughout the brain. a,b, Neuronal subclasses containing clusters that release modulatory neurotransmitters and their various co-release combinations with glutamate and/or GABA. UMAPs are coloured by subclass (a) and neurotransmitter type (b). c, Representative MERFISH sections showing the location of neuronal types expressing modulatory neurotransmitters.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读
### Panel a — subclass UMAP
**结论**：调节性递质亚类在taxonomy中的分布。
### Panel b — neurotransmitter UMAP
**结论**：展示递质及与Glut/GABA共释放组合。
### Panel c — MERFISH sections
**结论**：将多巴胺、5-HT、胆碱等细胞类型定位至脑区。

## 总体结论
递质类型及共释放模式呈现全脑空间异质性。
