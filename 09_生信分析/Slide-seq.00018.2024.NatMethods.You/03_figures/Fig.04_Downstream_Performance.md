# Fig. 4 — Comparison on Downstream Performance

## Caption（原文）
> Fig. 4 Comparison on downstream performance. a) Expression profiles generated by each platform were processed to obtain clustering results. Known cell types and states are colored in the left-most panel. Additionally, a schematic plot represents the expected cell states, arranged from outer space to inner space and from top to bottom. On the right-hand side, clustering results are presented, with spots color-coded by annotated cell states depicting the identifiable cell states. CM represents corneal mesenchyme; pNR represents presumptive neural retina; LV represents lens vesicle. b) Clustering was conducted on downsampled eye data from each platform, with an equal total read count across platforms in the eye area. The correspondence between annotations obtained from clustering based on all reads and clustering based on downsampled data is visualized in a heatmap. The number of spots in this correspondence is presented after log10 transformation without scaling. c) With Stereo-seq data as an example, spatial expression profiles of Aldh1a1 and Aldh1a3, which are expressed in pNR2 and pNR3 are shown at 10 µm resolution. The number of spots with the expression of Aldh1a1 above 0 is 1,329, and of Aldh1a3 above 0 is 217. d) Expression profiles of Aldh1a1 and Aldh1a3 in snRNA-seq data are presented, The number of cells having an expression of Aldh1a1 above 0 is 93, and of Aldh1a3 above 0 is 161. e) An overview of cell states compared in the marker gene detection analysis, with pNR4 and pNR1 highlighted in the top panel, and lens and melanocytes highlighted in the bottom panel. f) Number of marker genes detected with different numbers of reads used for each sST method in the comparison between pNR4 and pNR1 (top panel). The same analysis is applied to the lens and melanocytes in the bottom panel. g) In the top panel, an Upset plot displays the intersection of marker genes obtained by different sST methods using all reads for the pNR4 and pNR1 comparison. Genes shared among all three platforms are denoted in blue, those shared between two platforms are in purple, and uniquely obtained genes are represented in pink. The bottom panel presents a similar analysis for lens and melanocytes.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel 4a — Clustering Results by Platform
**结论**：Slide-seq V2和Stereo-seq能成功识别所有预期细胞亚群，而BMKMANU S1000和Visium存在检测局限性。

**关键数据**：
- Slide-seq V2和Stereo-seq：成功分离所有预期亚群
- BMKMANU S1000：难以检测黑色素细胞（与扩散有关）
- Visium：约75个spots/样本，空间分辨率限制详细亚群识别

### Panel 4b — Downsampling Impact on Clustering
**结论**：Downsampling后仍能检测到大部分细胞亚群，但相似表达谱的亚群（如神经视网膜各亚群）难以区分。

**关键数据**：
- ECA和ECP值较高，表明存在不一致性
- 主要影响：相似表达谱的细胞亚群混合

### Panel 4c,d — Stereo-seq vs snRNA-seq Resolution
**结论**：空间转录组数据在检测空间受限表达模式方面优于snRNA-seq。

**关键数据**：
- Stereo-seq Aldh1a1+: 1,329 spots
- snRNA-seq Aldh1a1+: 93 cells
- 空间数据提供了更精细的位置信息

### Panel 4e-g — Marker Gene Detection
**结论**：不同平台在标记基因检测方面各有独特和共享的标记基因，测序深度影响检测数量。

**关键数据**：
- 测序深度增加，检测到的标记基因数量增加
- 每个平台都有大量特异性标记基因
- 跨平台共享的标记基因数量有限

## 总体结论
Fig. 4展示了sST数据在下沉分析中的表现差异：空间转录组数据能够捕获snRNA-seq无法检测到的空间受限表达模式，但聚类和标记基因检测结果受平台特异性影响。
