# ED Fig. 5 — Cell-Type Spillover Index Across Panels

## Caption（原文）
> Extended Data Fig. 5 | Cell-type spillover index across panels and distribution of contamination level in doublets and singlets. a, Each dot represents the average Cosine similarity between a cell's secondary cell-type weight (w2) and the proportion of that same cell type in its spatial neighborhood, computed for a given cell-type pair across all Xenium samples profiled with the Breast, Custom IO and 5K panels. b, Cosine similarity between cell's secondary cell-type weight (w2) and the proportion of the secondary cell type in each cell's neighborhood, computed for all pairs of primary and secondary cell types across replicates.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel a — Breast/Custom IO/5K面板的溢出指数
**结论**：与其他面板一致，恶性细胞具有最高溢出指数，是主要污染源。

**关键数据**：
- Breast panel: Malignant cell最高
- Custom IO: 类似模式
- 5K panel: 较低但仍显著

### Panel b — 各细胞类型对的污染分布
**结论**：大多数细胞类型对的w2与邻域同类型比例呈正相关，证实溢出普遍存在。

**关键数据**：
- 仅显著相关的配对显示
- 双细胞中污染信号更强

## 总体结论
ED Fig. 5扩展验证了转录本溢出的普遍性，跨所有面板一致。
