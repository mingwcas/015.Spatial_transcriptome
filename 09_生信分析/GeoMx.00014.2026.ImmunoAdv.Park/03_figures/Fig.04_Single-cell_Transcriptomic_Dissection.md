# Fig. 4 — Single-cell Transcriptomic Dissection of Immune Composition and Macrophage Polarization

## Caption（原文）
> Single-cell transcriptomic dissection of immune composition, cytotoxicity, and macrophage polarization in metastatic dMMR CRC. (a) UMAP projection of single-cell transcriptomes showing the distribution of immune and non-immune cells across metastatic tissues. Immune cells are distinguished from epithelial and stromal compartments. (b) Relative proportions of annotated immune cell types. (c) Expression of representative cytotoxicity-associated genes in T and NK cells across metastatic sites. (d) Cytotoxicity scores compared between pembrolizumab-sensitive and -resistant groups. (e) UMAP embedding of macrophages highlighting distinct subpopulations defined by canonical marker genes. (f) Distribution of macrophage subpopulations across different metastatic sites. (g) Expression of M1- and M2-associated genes across tissues. (h) Module scores representing M1 and M2 polarization states, stratified by ICI response status.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel 4a — UMAP of Single Cells
**结论**：17,970个单细胞转录组UMAP可视化，鉴定出7个主要细胞群（T细胞、NK细胞、巨噬细胞、B细胞、浆细胞、上皮细胞、基质细胞）
**关键数据**：7个主要细胞compartment

### Panel 4b — Cell Type Proportions
**结论**：NK细胞在敏感灶（左卵巢、腹膜）中比例显著高于抵抗灶（右卵巢、网膜）
**关键数据**：NK细胞丰度与响应正相关

### Panel 4c-d — Cytotoxicity Gene Expression
**结论**：敏感灶T/NK细胞高表达细胞毒性基因（GZMB、PRF1、GNLY、IFNG、CD8A/B），细胞毒性评分显著更高
**关键数据**：P = .007

### Panel 4e — Macrophage UMAP
**结论**：巨噬细胞UMAP显示多个亚群，以SPP1+、TREM2+（M2型）和C1QC+、S100A8+（M1型）为标志
**关键数据**：M1/M2极化亚群分离

### Panel 4f — Macrophage Subset Distribution
**结论**：敏感灶中M2样亚群（SPP1+、TREM2+）显著减少
**关键数据**：左卵巢和腹膜中M2型减少

### Panel 4g — M1/M2 Gene Expression
**结论**：SPP1和TREM2（M2标志）在网膜中最高；C1QC和S100A8（M1标志）在敏感灶高表达
**关键数据**：M2基因在抵抗灶富集

### Panel 4h — Polarization Module Scores
**结论**：M1评分在敏感灶显著升高（P = 9.35e-36），M2评分在抵抗灶显著升高（P = 6.30e-19）
**关键数据**：极化状态与响应高度相关

## 总体结论
Fig. 4通过单细胞转录组解析揭示了细胞组成和功能差异：敏感灶富集NK细胞和高细胞毒性CD8+ T细胞，同时巨噬细胞向M1表型极化；抵抗灶则以M2样SPP1+ TAMs主导，形成免疫抑制微环境。
