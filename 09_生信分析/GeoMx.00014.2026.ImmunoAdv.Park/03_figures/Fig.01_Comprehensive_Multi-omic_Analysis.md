# Fig. 1 — Comprehensive Multi-omic Analysis and Imaging of Metastatic dMMR CRC

## Caption（原文）
> Comprehensive multi-omic analysis and imaging of metastatic dMMR CRC. Metastatic CRC with dMMR, involving multiple anatomical sites including the liver, peritoneum, ovary, and omentum, was characterized using an integrative multi-omic and spatial profiling approach. (a) Study overview depicting the integrated multi-omic design and corresponding tissue sampling sites. (b) Schematic representation of metastatic sites with associated sample types and analytical platforms. (c) Preoperative CT imaging showing bilateral ovarian metastases prior to immune checkpoint blockade. (d) Postoperative evaluation of metastatic lesions following four cycles of pembrolizumab, including representative tissue image (left) and matched CT scan (right). Labels such as left ovary and right ovary indicate anatomical origin of lesions and are not related to the vertical placement of images.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel 1a — Study Overview
**结论**：展示本研究的多组学整合策略和样本采集方案
**关键数据**：5个转移部位（结肠、肝、腹膜、左卵巢、右卵巢）同步取样

### Panel 1b — Metastatic Sites Schematic
**结论**：各转移灶对应样本类型和分析平台的示意图
**关键数据**：每个部位分别进行Bulk RNA-seq、scRNA-seq、mIHC多组学检测

### Panel 1c — Pre-treatment CT Imaging
**结论**：治疗前CT显示双侧卵巢转移灶
**关键数据**：Pembrolizumab治疗前影像

### Panel 1d — Post-treatment Evaluation
**结论**：4周期Pembrolizumab后各转移灶的治疗反应评估
**关键数据**：腹膜和左卵巢显示消退，右卵巢和网膜显示持续存在

## 总体结论
Fig. 1确立了研究框架：通过整合Bulk RNA-seq、scRNA-seq和mIHC三种技术平台，对dMMR CRC患者的5个转移灶进行多组学分析，揭示了对Pembrolizumab的异质性响应（敏感：腹膜+左卵巢；抵抗：右卵巢+网膜+结肠）。
