# Fig. 2 — Functional Characterization of Immune Heterogeneity and Checkpoint Blockade Responsiveness

## Caption（原文）
> Functional characterization of immune heterogeneity and checkpoint blockade responsiveness in metastatic dMMR CRC. (a) Functional transcriptomic profiling of metastatic tumor samples stratified by immune infiltration status (immune high vs. immune low). (b) Volcano plots highlighting differentially expressed genes between immune-high and immune-low tumors. (c) PCA reveals distinct clustering of samples based on pembrolizumab sensitivity, distinguishing sensitive from resistant phenotypes. (d, e) Expression patterns of key immune-associated genes were evaluated across distinct treatment response groups (f) Multiplex immunohistochemistry (mIHC) analysis of metastatic lesions using epithelial (CK) and immune markers (PD-L1, CD8, CD68, CD163, and CD11c), displaying integrated tissue maps (far left) and marker-specific distributions for each region.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel 2a — Transcriptomic Profiling by Immune Status
**结论**：敏感肿瘤灶（腹膜、左卵巢）富集抗肿瘤免疫基因特征，包括效应T细胞和NK细胞活性模块
**关键数据**：免疫高浸润组 vs 免疫低浸润组

### Panel 2b — Volcano Plots DEGs
**结论**：免疫高组上调基因富集于淋巴细胞迁移和激活通路（CXCL12、CD28、TNFRSF8）
**关键数据**：免疫低组富集代谢和染色质调控基因（HELLS、HMGCS1、NEIL3）

### Panel 2c — PCA Clustering
**结论**：PCA显示敏感组（左卵巢+腹膜）聚类在一起，与抵抗组（结肠、肝、右卵巢）明显分离
**关键数据**：第一主成分区分免疫治疗响应状态

### Panel 2d-e — Immune Gene Expression
**结论**：敏感组高表达免疫检查点分子（CD274/PD-L1、PDCD1LG2、LAG3、TIGIT）和细胞毒性基因（GZMA、PRF1、GNLY）
**关键数据**：敏感组CD274表达显著高于抵抗组

### Panel 2f — mIHC Tissue Maps
**结论**：多标免疫组化展示各转移灶的肿瘤-免疫空间分布
**关键数据**：CK+PD-L1+肿瘤细胞、CD8+ T细胞、CD68+/CD163+巨噬细胞分布

## 总体结论
Fig. 2揭示了免疫治疗响应与肿瘤免疫微环境的关联：敏感灶表现为免疫激活状态（高细胞毒性、IFN-γ信号、CD8+ T细胞浸润），而抵抗灶则呈现免疫沉默表型。PCA分析证实转录组特征能够区分响应状态。
