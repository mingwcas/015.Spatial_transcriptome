# Fig. 6 — Integrated Spatial–Molecular Model of Metastatic dMMR CRC and Pembrolizumab Response

## Caption（原文）
> Integrated spatial–molecular model of metastatic dMMR CRC and pembrolizumab response. (a) Schematic overview of primary colon carcinoma dissemination to four metastatic niches—peritoneum, omentum, left ovary, and right ovary—prior to therapy, and the subsequent divergence into pembrolizumab-sensitive (peritoneum and left ovary) and -resistant (omentum and right ovary) phenotypes following treatment. In sensitive lesions, CK+PD-L1+ tumor cells are tightly colocalized with CD68+ M1-like macrophages and CD8+ T cells, exhibit elevated cytolytic activity, IFN-γ signaling, and MHC-I/II-mediated antigen presentation, and spatially exclude CD163+ M2-like macrophages. By contrast, resistant lesions are characterized by CK+PD-L1+–CD163+ proximity, restricted CD8+ T-cell infiltration, and enrichment of immunosuppressive cytokines and matrix-remodeling factors (e.g. MMPs), encapsulating site-specific ICI efficacy in dMMR CRC.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel 6a — Integrated Model Overview
**结论**：原发结肠癌向4个转移部位播散（腹膜、网膜、双侧卵巢），经Pembrolizumab治疗后分化为敏感（腹膜+左卵巢）和抵抗（网膜+右卵巢）表型
**关键数据**：同一起源，不同响应

### Sensitive Lesion Features (peritoneum, left ovary)
**结论**：敏感灶特征包括：
- CK+PD-L1+肿瘤细胞与CD68+ M1样巨噬细胞和CD8+ T细胞紧密共定位
- 高细胞毒性活性、IFN-γ信号、MHC-I/II抗原提呈
- 空间排斥CD163+ M2样巨噬细胞

**关键数据**：效应细胞-肿瘤近距离、免疫激活信号

### Resistant Lesion Features (omentum, right ovary)
**结论**：抵抗灶特征包括：
- CK+PD-L1+与CD163+ M2巨噬细胞近距离
- CD8+ T细胞浸润受限
- 免疫抑制细胞因子和基质重塑因子（MMPs）富集

**关键数据**：免疫抑制空间架构

## 总体结论
Fig. 6是该研究的整合模型：同一dMMR CRC原发灶来源的转移灶在免疫检查点抑制剂治疗后呈现二元响应模式。敏感灶保持免疫激活的空间架构（效应细胞-肿瘤近距离+M1极化），而抵抗灶建立免疫抑制微环境（M2主导+空间隔离效应T细胞）。这一框架为dMMR CRC的精准免疫治疗提供了理论依据。
