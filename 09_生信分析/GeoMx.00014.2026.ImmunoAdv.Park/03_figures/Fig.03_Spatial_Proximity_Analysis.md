# Fig. 3 — Spatial Proximity Analysis of PD-L1+ Tumor Cells and Immune Subsets

## Caption（原文）
> Spatial proximity analysis of PD-L1+ tumor cells and immune subsets in metastatic dMMR CRC. (a) Representative multiplex immunohistochemistry (mIHC) images depicting spatial localization of PD-L1+ epithelial tumor cells (CK+PD-L1+), cytotoxic T cells (CD8+), and M2-like macrophages (CD163+) across metastatic regions. DAPI, CK, PD-L1, CD8, CD68, CD163, and CD11c markers were used for segmentation and phenotyping. Nearest distances from CK+PD-L1+ tumor cells to CD8+ and CD163+ immune cells are visualized with white connecting lines. (b–e) Quantitative spatial analyses measuring the nearest distances (in µm) between immune and tumor compartments across distinct metastatic sites. (b) Distance from CK+PD-L1+ cells to CD68+CD11c+ myeloid cells. (c) Distance from CK+PD-L1+ cells to CD68+CD11+ cells. (d) Distance from CK+PD-L1+ cells to CD8+ T cells. (e) Distance from CD8+ T cells to CD163+ macrophages. (f) Distance from CD8+ T cells to CD68+CD11c⁻ macrophages.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel 3a — mIHC Representative Images
**结论**：展示各转移灶的mIHC空间分布，PD-L1+肿瘤细胞与CD8+ T细胞、CD163+ M2巨噬细胞的空间关系
**关键数据**：白色连线标记最近邻距离

### Panel 3b-c — Tumor-Myeloid Cell Distance
**结论**：敏感灶（腹膜、左卵巢）中CK+PD-L1+肿瘤细胞与M1样巨噬细胞（CD68+CD11c+）距离更近
**关键数据**：腹膜和左卵巢的最近邻距离显著短于抵抗灶

### Panel 3d — Tumor-CD8+ T Cell Distance
**结论**：敏感灶中肿瘤细胞与CD8+ T细胞距离更近，提示增强的免疫监视
**关键数据**：敏感组距离显著缩短

### Panel 3e-f — CD8+ T Cell-Macrophage Distance
**结论**：敏感灶中CD8+ T细胞远离CD163+ M2型和CD68+CD11c-巨噬细胞，提示空间排斥免疫抑制细胞
**关键数据**：抵抗灶中这些距离更短

## 总体结论
Fig. 3证实了空间邻近性在免疫治疗响应中的关键作用：敏感灶表现为肿瘤细胞与效应免疫细胞（CD8+ T细胞、M1巨噬细胞）的近距离接触，同时空间排斥免疫抑制细胞（M2巨噬细胞）。这一空间架构模式是响应预测的重要指标。
