# Fig. 5 — Spatial CNs correlated with LNM and survival

## Caption（原文）
> (A) Spatial maps (left) of CN assignment in representative PT, PT-LNM, and LNMT samples. (Right) Corresponding Voronoi diagram representations of the CNs. Scale bars, 100 μm. (B) Heatmaps showing the cellular composition within each identified CN across PT, PT-LNM, and LNMT, calculated based on the composition of the N = 10 nearest neighboring cells. A total of 11, 13, and 15 CNs were identified in PT, PT-LNM, and LNMT, respectively. (C) Pie plots showing the relative frequency of the six major CNs in PT, PT-LNM, and LNMT. Boxplots comparing the log2-transformed percentage abundance of specific CNs among PT, PT-LNM, and LNMT. Data are represented as the median and IQR, and whiskers indicate maximum and minimum values. The p values were calculated using two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. (D) Forest plots from univariable Cox regression analysis showing the HR and 95% confidence interval for each CN's association with OS and DFS. (E) Survival analysis based on PIHs-1 abundance. Kaplan-Meier curves for OS (left) and DFS (right) of 73 SCLC patients stratified into high (>median) and low (≤median) PIHs-1 abundance groups. p values were calculated using the log rank test.

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — CN空间分布图
**结论**：每个样本中细胞被分配到不同的细胞邻里(CN)，Voronoi图展示空间组织结构
**关键数据**：PT: 11 CNs; PT-LNM: 13 CNs; LNMT: 15 CNs

### Panel B — CN细胞组成热图
**结论**：CNs被归纳为6种主要空间结构：肿瘤compartment、exhausted niche、PIHs-1、PIHs-2、B cell-enriched niche、血管niche；C5、C6、C9富集的CNs主要在PT-LNM和LNMT中
**关键数据**：N=10最近邻细胞定义局部微环境

### Panel C — CN丰度比较
**结论**：PIHs-1在PT中最高(中位数1.458%)，PT-LNM(0.294%)，LNMT(0.0197%)；PT-LNM富集exhausted niche(中位数6.510%)
**关键数据**：Wilcoxon检验显著性

### Panel D — CN丰度与生存
**结论**：PIHs-1高丰度与良好OS和DFS显著相关；Exhausted niche与不良预后相关
**关键数据**：Univariable Cox回归HR和95% CI

### Panel E — PIHs-1 Kaplan-Meier曲线
**结论**：PIHs-1高组3年/5年生存率(OS: 88%/73.2%)显著高于低组(OS: 71.3%/45.2%)；多变量Cox回归证实PIHs-1是独立预后因素
**关键数据**：Log-rank p = 0.006 (OS), p = 0.014 (DFS); OS: HR=4.418, 95%CI: 1.415-13.795, Cox p=0.011

## 总体结论
Fig. 5揭示了空间细胞组织结构与淋巴结转移和患者预后的关联。PIHs-1作为多细胞协同定位形成的泛免疫热点，是独立预后因素，其空间架构而非单纯细胞组成决定临床预后。
