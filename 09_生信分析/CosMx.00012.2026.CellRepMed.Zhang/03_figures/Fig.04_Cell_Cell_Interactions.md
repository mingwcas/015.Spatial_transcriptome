# Fig. 4 — Single-cell spatial analysis unravels cell-cell co-localization profiles

## Caption（原文）
> (A) Schematic illustrating the cell-cell co-localization across PT, PT-LNM, and LNMT. Scale bars: 100 μm (overview) and 6 μm (detail, right panels). (B) Heatmaps summarizing significant pairwise interactions (red) or avoidance (blue) between all cell types in PT (top), PT-LNM (middle), and LNMT (bottom). (C) Heatmaps of Spearman's correlation coefficients for pairwise cell type abundances. Red indicates positive correlation and blue indicates negative correlation. (D) Boxplots comparing the expression of CTLA-4, GZMA, and GZMB in effector CD4+ T cells that are spatially interacting with (red) or avoiding (blue) endothelial cells. Data are represented as the median and interquartile range (IQR), and whiskers indicate maximum and minimum values. The p values from two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests; ns denotes not significant (p > 0.05).

## Panel-by-Panel 解读

### Panel A — 细胞共定位示意图
**结论**：展示了恶性细胞与免疫细胞之间、及内皮细胞与免疫细胞之间的空间相互作用概念图
**关键数据**：scale bars: 100μm (overview), 6μm (detail)

### Panel B — 相互作用/回避热图
**结论**：PT中恶性细胞与多种非癌细胞类型有10对相互作用；PT-LNM中仅5对；C5、C6、C9亚群表现出免疫回避特征；内皮细胞与所有恶性亚群均回避，但与多种免疫细胞类型呈正向相互作用
**关键数据**：红=相互作用，蓝=回避；共396对细胞类型组合

### Panel C — 细胞类型丰度相关性
**结论**：C5、C6、C9亚群丰度与免疫细胞浸润呈正相关，与空间回避行为一致(局部免疫排除是空间调控现象)
**关键数据**：Spearman相关性热图

### Panel D — 血管旁T细胞功能标记
**结论**：PT中血管旁效应CD4+ T细胞表达高CTLA-4；PT-LNM和LNMT中血管旁效应CD4+ T细胞表达高GZMA和GZMB(细胞毒性标记)
**关键数据**：Wilcoxon检验：CTLA-4 (p=0.054 ns); GZMA (p=0.063 ns); GZMB (PT vs LNMT p=0.012)

## 总体结论
Fig. 4揭示了血管-免疫niche的空间动态重编程：内皮细胞通过回避恶性细胞同时与免疫细胞形成功能性的血管旁niche，在淋巴结转移过程中，血管旁免疫细胞从CTLA-4+表型转变为细胞毒性GZMA/B+表型。
